# Redwall — a home made perceptible through work, appetite and voice

`DESIGN-READ-RW-001` · Separate design-reading pass · Targeted primary-text evidence, not a complete new sequential reading. This document concerns reusable direction; it does not retell the adventure. Exact extraction locators resolve in this package's source audit.

## Emotional and stylistic direction

The useful emotional unit is an **ordinary place with something happening in it**. The orchard is smelled, napped in and seasonally tended; the pond has overnight fishing lines; a kitchen prepares tomorrow's breakfast while supplying tonight's workers. Home is tangible before any heroic symbol explains it. Build recurring routines into a room's presentation and job interfaces: food cooling, jugs being filled, a keeper checking stores, a table changing from preparation to service. [RW-D01–03, RW-D09]

Jacques's accumulating food lists create anticipation through texture, preparation and sequence. Steam arrives before the celebrated dish, a cook makes an entrance, the Abbot tastes, and the guests react. This is staged collective attention. A game feast should have readable preparation, arrival, service and response; it should not be represented solely by a counter increment. The same technique can introduce a repaired bell, a finished room or a returning expedition. [RW-D01]

Humor often comes from elaborate self-presentation meeting affectionate familiarity. Basil's formal introductions, inflated appetite and sudden seriousness differ from Hugo's culinary ceremony and Cornflower's quiet participation in a shared joke. Distinguish voices through rhythm, priorities and relationships before spelling dialect heavily. Keep the operational UI plain under DEC-017. [RW-D04, RW-D08]

The source's grace, bowed heads and communal response are explicit ritual evidence. Do not describe the Abbey as culturally blank. The Refuge's adopted locally authored Charter can have original shared customs without automatically copying this religious form. Ambrose's sleight-of-hand also shows that playful performance and narrated wonder can coexist; the narrator's jocular claim of magic does not settle the game's metaphysics. [RW-D01]

## Additional game-direction opportunities

Use sound to make occupied spaces understandable: a distant bell, chairs moving, kitchen vessels and changing work noises. Use warm light and maintained surfaces without making every day a festival. Keep public ceremony and private care at different scales. Dialogue can respond to a known relationship or current task; it does not require a new personality simulator to be specific.

The source contains early goatsmilk, cheese, eggs, explicit religious language, exaggerated flexible tail use and harmful conduct by sympathetic characters. Preserve these as source facts while applying the game's diet, anatomy and content decisions. Do not use a source feast to justify adding livestock or a punitive care mechanic.
