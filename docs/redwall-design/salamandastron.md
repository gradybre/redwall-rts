## Theme, style and feeling

This book offers a useful contrast of inhabited textures: sunny sandstone, kitchen heat, dark mountain corridors and a flower-framed domestic cave. The strength of a place is measured partly by whether it feeds and shelters people. A military mountain must therefore contain a plausible domestic program; a cave dwelling can be spacious, deliberately furnished and welcoming. [SA-D01, SA-D05–06]

Food descriptions alternate between theatrical abundance and exact stages of work. Friar Bellows supervises several preparations, corrects an apprentice and explains how a cake is assembled after baking and cooling. These stages suggest excellent art and job-state references. They do not establish input masses or a production rate. Culinary confidence in a narrator is not equivalent to a complete crafting contract. [SA-D03]

The broader care study remains useful: laundry, water, cleaning, ingredient gathering and retained knowledge all matter during the Abbey illness. Read the separate earlier SA-P03–05 for those scenes. This pass adds domestic objects and room relationships to that systems direction. Preserve the user's serious consequences without turning fictional remedies into medical instructions.

## Locations and map composition

Salamandastron's inspected interior sequence descends through armory rooms, dormitories, a spiral stair and dining room to kitchens with an adjoining storeroom. Treat this as a source traversal sequence, not a universal floor plan or proof that every level has only one use. Sound is muffled inside the rock. [SA-D05]

The island home has carved window openings, trailing plants, woven rush floor coverings, dead-log seats with barkcloth covers, a rock hearth/oven and a slab table. These are direct environmental modeling references for an inhabited cave: reachable sill, usable seat, work surface and a route from entrance wash trough to table. No canonical metric dimensions are supplied. [SA-D06]

The shrew account connects a tributary of the Great South Stream with a vast lake and central island. It is narrated remembered travel, not a measured world map. The feared white apparition has a particular identity; do not generalize that explanation to all uncertain wonder. [SA-D04]

## Further systems opportunities

Represent kitchens as workplaces with hot crowded periods and quieter external dining spaces. Store household food customs in content metadata; keep character preferences distinct from species requirements. Ordinary swimming needs valid bank access and route capacity; island visits by boat are a different transport mechanism. The source's dangerous medicine, poison and slapstick discipline remain references to interpret, not instructions to add unapproved systems.
