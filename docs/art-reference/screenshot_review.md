# Screenshot-by-screenshot reference review

Document `SET-ART-REF-001`, revision 1.0, 2026-09-06. All 28 original PNGs were opened individually and visually inspected, including their visible text. Ordering follows screenshot timestamps, not an inferred slide-deck sequence. IMG-02 and IMG-28 repeat the same specialist slide with different framing; both were inspected. No missing slide, hidden carousel panel or cropped anatomy is claimed as reviewed.

`OBSERVED` describes the screenshot. `DIRECT_REFERENCE` identifies features to consult while modeling. `PROPOSED` is our adaptation or production choice. A depicted ability is evidence of this presentation's proposal, not proof of novel canon, an implemented game, or Brendan's acceptance of every feature. Creators, original slide source and individual illustration editions are unidentified. Preserve that uncertainty; do not invent artist attribution or call an image AI-generated from appearance.

Each original is linked below. Selection windows and file digests are in `reference_manifest.json`. Windows identify useful areas for inspection; they are authored viewing guides, not pixel-accurate masks or anatomical measurements. Screen overlays and carousel arrows are not part of any model.

**Observed art.** A standing hare holds an oversized mustard-gold banner and a narrow sword. Long unequal ear poses, a forward muzzle, white cheek tufts, large bare hind feet and a tall narrow silhouette preserve species identity. The green, buttoned, high-collared coat has gold edging, shoulder boards, medals and a brown belt. The ears and banner create two strong vertical shapes; the flag's broad fold masses carry more visual weight than its faint insignia.

**Direct model reference.** Use the whole figure for the hare's standing silhouette and the coat-to-leg relationship; use the hand/shaft and sword hand for separate grip studies. Build ears as articulated tapered volumes with an inner surface. Open the coat below the belt to permit stride; keep feet and ear tips outside clothing. Model the flag pole, cloth and finial separately. Preserve the large cloth folds in a baked motion rather than requiring crowd cloth simulation. Medal geometry can collapse into atlas detail at distance. The rear coat, belt routing, flag attachment and sword scabbard are not visible and need original construction drawings.

**Mechanics and application.** The slide proposes morale/melee-defense support, charge defense, a Schiltron formation affecting the bearer’s unit and up to four others, and a stronger lord aura. Preserve the recognizable standard-bearer role for later battle planning. Settlement equivalents are visible messengers, stewards and ceremonial carriers, each with a concrete duty. The aura, five-unit reach, formation unlock and period-coded military uniform are reference choices, not adopted rules or a universal Long Patrol dress code. Compare IMG-27 before choosing hare clothing.
