The explicit cases in frozen-witnesses.json precede implementation. Their expected codes come from the candidate contract; this is planning data, not a Python model used as an engine oracle. Fixtures additionally cover every physical habitat/stock row, each of22 field extents at zero/short/long, typed bucket shapes, full32/96 capacity, exact priority and packed-buffer nonmutation.

Required semantic mutation units: all22 canonical projections separately; all5 byte-field guards separately; habitat enum, each3 scalar domains, present self/zone shapes and inactive self/zone shapes; all4 inactive-state relations (capacity, effort_used, intensive, permitted retained effort); all3 present habitat relations (effort_slots, effort_used range, capacity); stock presence relation, inactive-stock blank group, stock owner-reference equality, species nonnegative range; stock capacity, population lower/upper, harvested lower/upper bounds; strict below30 and above40 hysteresis constraints; within-habitat species uniqueness and total quota; self-slot uniqueness and exact zone-pair uniqueness.
