## Direction that matters most

Everyday skills drive the plot: nursing, cooking, gathering, record keeping, boat repair, singing, wordplay, cellar expertise, insect observation and reciprocal hospitality. Grath's vengeance and Inbar's inexperience expose the difference between target skill and willingness to kill. Romsca's protection of captives and Fermald's friendship with Graylunk challenge faction-wide moral assumptions without deleting prior crimes.

Piknim's death permanently changes the sound of Craklyn's singing and the rhythms of meals. The community makes room for grief, then communal cooking and new responsibilities. Gerul's flight prognosis is overturned later; Clecky's lost ear persists as a cloth adaptation. Neither should be flattened into a generic full-heal rule.

Treasure is deliberately relinquished. Tansy's decision values life above possession; the Abbot's dream-based transfer of ownership from Grath's kin is a source ethical ambiguity that requires explicit adaptation. Leadership is supported by mentors, harvest storage precedes feasting, and older residents find new satisfying roles.
