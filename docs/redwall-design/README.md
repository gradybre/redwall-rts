## 1. How to use this package

Start with this guide, then the relevant individual study, [content catalog](content_catalog.md), [location atlas](location_atlas.md) and [authoring handoff](authoring_handoff.md). Every study is organized around game direction rather than adventure chronology. The prior `docs/redwall-series/` package remains a separate source for plot-dependent character arcs, contradictions and ethical questions. The earlier complete supplied-text *Lord Brocktree* study remains intact.

This pass is substantial thematic sampling, **not twelve complete sequential rereadings or an exhaustive concordance of every item and character**. The full text of *Eulalia!* is not in the verified supplied corpus; its earlier excerpt-based evidence remains limited. The EPUB advertised as a collection contains *Lord Brocktree*, not twenty books. Source edition integrity is not certified. The package does not reproduce novel text or song lyrics.

The source hierarchy remains Redwall first, current project second, woodland-colony to fill gaps. Adopted project decisions control adaptations: scenario-specific roles/casts, recognizable anatomy, light dialect, varied dwellings, ordinary community life, serious grief, rare meaningful wonder with uncertain truth, plant food plus the named nonsapient-fish whitelist, and the user's content boundaries. Novel examples can inform these choices or expose a deliberate difference; they cannot silently reverse them.

## 3. Game-direction findings

### DIR-01 — Make home legible through action

A place feels inhabited when an observer can see what people do there: a keeper checks barrels, a cook sets a quiet breakfast aside, somebody repairs a coverlet, an elder remembers a garden's earlier keepers. Start a scene with a practical action and the relationship it expresses. Avoid making every resident an idle figure waiting for orders. [RT-D01; TR-D04; TG-D07]

For Rowan's Refuge, the experienced keeper should have a recognizable service routine and particular relationships. The user has not yet authored the founding cause, route of displacement or complete neighbor history. These findings support how to portray the keeper; they do not supply that biography.

### DIR-02 — Let meals have different purposes

Meals can welcome strangers, sustain night workers, restore choice, commemorate the dead, share expertise, celebrate harvest or simply feed a tired friend. A feast's purpose changes its staging and dialogue. The source also depicts scarcity and bad meals. Rich culinary identity needs ordinary bread and soup as well as spectacle. [RW-D09; MX-D01; MW-D05; PL-D09–10]

Render ingredients, preparation, serving and cleanup coherently. A tray supplied to a nightwatch resident should correspond to a committed delivery if it represents simulated food. Ambient staging cannot create an extra meal or duplicate inventory. New cuisine proposals need the owning balance rules before activation.

### DIR-03 — Culture crosses species; individuals cross roles

Polleekin's tree home, Grumm's appetite for fiery soup, Firjak's changed movement, Broggle's sewing and Skarlath's cheese-making all complicate simple archetypes. Distinct anatomy remains valuable, but species is not a complete occupation, home, preference or morality. Store those concerns separately. [MW-D03, MW-D06; OC-D03–04; TG-D07]

The Patrol, Guosim, Abbey, particular holts, traveling players and northern courts offer cultural variation. They are institutions and communities with histories, not interchangeable race bonuses. Characters who share an animal species can differ in manners, training, food and loyalties.

### DIR-04 — Give places topological and social depth

Brockhall's furnished roots, Polleekin's accessible tree, the mountain's domestic rooms and the Abbey's neglected attics provide concrete spatial programs. A room has an entrance, neighbors, work, light, storage and occupants. The same cave kit can serve a refuge, tomb or workshop only if its context changes. [MF-D04; MW-D03; SA-D05–06; PL-D02–03]

The accepted movement direction makes these relationships functional. A character above another is not reachable by proximity; a tunnel under a river is not submerged unless explicitly connected to water. Modeling briefs need clearance and transition anchors as well as surface appearance.

### DIR-05 — Keep quiet and public scales of emotion

Private care, a remembered object and a shared performance are different emotional forms. Grief does not forbid later humor; humor does not prove recovery. A former warrior can become an elder whose priorities have changed. Use context-specific, original behavior and dialogue; do not derive a universal grief timer from narrative pace. [MX-D03, MX-D08; TG-D04; earlier TG-P04–06]

### DIR-06 — Material history creates memorable objects

A spoon, monocle, doorstop, banner, boat or carved leaf becomes important through who made, used, gave, kept or misunderstood it. Give an object provenance before assigning spectacle. A recovered communal symbol need not grant a percentage bonus to matter. Keep object identity distinct from display name so gifts, renaming and succession can preserve history. [PL-D05; TG-D04–05; OC-D04; TR-D08]

### DIR-07 — Use sensory specificity and restrained dialogue

Jacques repeatedly juxtaposes warm food/light with weather, stone, water, leaf shadow and darkness. Use those relationships in art and sound. A hall at breakfast differs from that hall during a performance or a quiet vigil. Voices can differ through directness, formality, pace, favorite comparisons and concern for others. Use light dialect in dialogue, plain language in operational UI, and original text/music throughout. [SA-D01; LP-D04; RT-D04; TR-D04]

### DIR-08 — Preserve uncertainty without obscuring player state

A remembered tale, a dream, a character's hypothesis and a described mechanism have different evidence status. The Walking Stone receives a particular explanation; that does not disprove every vision. Nor does a sacred object automatically carry a functioning magical modifier. Keep narrative belief attributed while showing inventory, route, hazard and task state truthfully. [RT-D11; LB-D10; PL-D08]

## 5. What this pass originated

The comparative principles, content IDs, adaptation classifications, authoring schema, atlas diagrams and implementation gates are authored design work. Novel dishes, described materials and observed relationships are inherited source evidence with locators. Existing project limits remain inherited. No new production recipe quantity, nutrition effect, movement speed, oxygen capacity, body dimension or performance result was invented. Missing numerical contracts are expressly blocked from runtime activation rather than represented by placeholder logic.
