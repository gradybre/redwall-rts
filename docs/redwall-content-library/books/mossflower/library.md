## Direction: what this book contributes

Resistance succeeds through a whole community: kitchens, refugees, teachers, builders, scouts, farmers, caregivers, boat crews and fighters. The emotional center is the possibility of ordinary life free from extraction and intimidation. Model warmth through meals, chores, family care, shared jokes and remembered stories. Let military victories leave injuries, mourning, damaged terrain and work still to do.

The source alternates closely observed domestic comfort with frightening predation and authoritarian cruelty. Gonff’s cheerful performance eases tension, but Martin’s near-fatal injury and Mask’s death resist a consequence-free adventure tone. Bella’s mercy is a positive foundation; source bullying of captives and deception of children remain tensions to adapt deliberately.

Anatomy supports capabilities without imposing absolute species classes: moles solve earth problems, bats make coordinated aerial rescues, otters move passengers in water, squirrels carry mice through trees, and Amber is remembered rescuing Skipper from the pool. Terrain material and route conditions matter: sand cannot be treated like tunnel-supporting loam, a river route can hide a waterfall, and flood level can invalidate an otherwise correct diversion plan.

## Canon-to-game decisions that must remain explicit

| Evidence | Design consequence |
|---|---|
| Snakefish, a giant eel, speaks and bargains (2695 and subsequent escape). | Conflicts with the current nonsapient-fish rule. Choose an exceptional named character, a rewritten encounter, or a project rule change explicitly; do not silently classify him as food. |
| Milk, cheese, cream and butter appear throughout; shrimp, bream, limpet and woodpigeon also appear as food. | Preserve canon dish names. Game versions use named plant substitutes or permitted fish/mussel variants and carry AI-authorship labels. |
| Nightshade and other plants appear in emergency treatment (4935–4969). | Retain a nonactionable fictional healer reference; never turn the source into validated medical instructions. |
| Earlier male-line lore contrasts with Spearlady Gorse in the mountain chronicle (3509). | Mark the tradition as a claim with counterevidence; do not prohibit female badger leaders on this basis. |
| Brogg/Scratt change ferret/weasel descriptions; Thicktail changes weasel/stoat. | Keep source ambiguity until edition comparison; do not silently choose species. |
| Brogg’s curved sword is broken (4521), then present again (4707). | Record an unexplained replacement or continuity problem. |
| Flooding seeks to reduce deaths yet drowns defenders and destroys Kotir. | Make displacement, rescue and collateral consequences visible; do not describe the operation as bloodless. |
| The engine is called a ballista but described with throwing arm/counterweight. | Asset naming may retain the literary term while mechanics use the actual depicted structure. |
| Redwall building and quarry freight are planned before the frame confirms later completion. | Keep construction milestones and chronology separate. |
