## Evidence rules

Recipes retain actual dishes, proposals, remembered meals, preferences, cooking songs and comic imagined foods separately. A cooking song can supply actual preparation evidence (hodgepodge); a comic sea monologue cannot establish historical people or served food. All inferred pantry bases and substitutions are labeled AI_AUTHORED_GAME_ADAPTATION, inactive and unbalanced. Narrative counts do not establish simulation yields, timing or work costs.

The source's cheese and cream origin is generally unspecified; no dairy pipeline is inferred. Explicit oat preparations are authored game choices. Greensap milk is not automatically animal milk. Unsafe or ambiguous botanical claims and poison references remain source-only, with no real remedy or dosing instructions. Fish outside the project whitelist are preserved as source references and either excluded or assigned explicitly renamed game versions. Pike and barbel are hazards, not player food.

## Direction that matters most

Marlfox gives the game a strong account of community recovery: repairing windows is only one part of mending. Grief, bad memories, family shame, lost companions and exhausted caregivers need attention. Rusvul's harmful expectations are challenged, and his apology matters. Dippler's eventual leadership explicitly rejects bullying young members. Cregga refuses formal rule while mentoring others; Brimm and Sollertree value chosen solitude. Adults continue learning, as Rimrose becomes an apprentice recorder.

Power works through spectacle, coercion and information control. Lantur's current White Ghost is explicitly a constructed sheet illusion; subjective Martin dreams remain separate evidence. Mokkan's successor regime reproduces terror. The final water rats become peaceful farmers, a direct source counterexample to immutable species morality. Preserve that distinction when authoring factions.

Craft and movement form a connected world: covered carts, mud-brick houses under rock roofs, carved bank caves, osier/bark/resin boats, portable hulls, inhabited rafts, wallgates, rooftop access and underground escape tunnels. Distinct terrain transitions matter. A swimmer can drown in a torrent, a bird can need ground travel, and a faster boat can lose to more rowers with local current knowledge. Cooperative rescue and guides are core systems, not decorative events.

## Contradictions and limits requiring preservation

- Castle Marl is explicitly all ramps/no stairs (456,3226), yet earlier scenes use stairs terminology (1586,1938,2334). Use ramps for the coherent game layout and flag the textual conflict.
- Cregga's reconstructed Urthstripe/Urthwyte account (781) requires comparison with Salamandastron; do not silently replace either source.
- The riddle names fish of blue and gray, while its revealed landmark is one gray fish-shaped rock with a blue-flowered eye (3006).
- Fourteen adopted hedgehogs are described (2893), while later detachments can read as five on Swallow plus ten with Gawjo. Do not invent an extra named family member.
- Megraw's presumed broken wing is later diagnosed as dislocated and flight returns. Nutwing dies; Cregga's lost ear is not restored. No universal healing rule follows.
- Named recipe libraries can be comprehensive against a fallible full reading without asserting every edition or every possible repeated mention is indexed. Generic ten-cheese/twelve-bread assortment (3451) does not name twenty-two canonical varieties.
