Read-only discovery during Farming CI and Fishing implementation. No accepted Movement validation contract or production edit. SET-MOVE-001 remains authoritative: this baseline ground-motion store does not complete swimming, diving, tunnels, canopy access, occupied transitions or the MOVE-G01–05 gates. Historical source-header claims about missing ledger entries or other owners must be checked against the current repository before being repeated as current blockers.

Owner8 movement version1 has primary512, zero child extents,16 i32[512] fields at global begin171. Value bytes32768, payload32900, block32932, offset9246459. Canonical order vx,vz,remainder_x,remainder_z,next_x,next_z,correction_x,correction_z,radius_u,desired_yaw,next_yaw,grid_next,grid_cell,speed_u_per_s,movement_phase,blocked_ticks. These are distinct from nine route/admission cursor fields in section2 and saved profile/navigation/contact state elsewhere. No complete public sixteen-column capture/apply exists. Cold defaults are all zero except grid_next/grid_cell=-1.

Only zero writers exist for correction_x/z, radius_u, desired_yaw, next_yaw and blocked_ticks: allocation and `_reset_motion`. They are reserved in the current baseline, not permission to infer body clearances, facing or separation policy. Movement phase is one of IDLE0/TRAVELLING1/ARRIVED2/ROUTE_LOST3/PROFILE_STALE4/CONTACT_STALE5. Remainders are0..419 through denominator30*14; stop/reset zero them, terminal `_settle` retains them. Speed comes from Residents.SIZE_MOVEMENT_U_PER_S=[3277,4096,3072], or zero before first admission. Stop does not clear speed or grid_cell; inactive resident slots may retain old motion and must not be force-cleared from current live population.

Grid cells are -1 or0..262143; stored targets are either both0 before target/reset, or actual source cell centres256..261888 at512 spacing. TRAVELLING requires valid grid_cell/grid_next and exact target centre of grid_next. All terminal phases clear grid_next; they keep prior grid_cell and target coordinates. An immediately completed one-cell route can be ARRIVED with target0 and valid grid_cell. A normal completed route keeps its final target centre. Source scan suggests terminal phases other than IDLE require positive source speed and valid grid_cell; confirm before freezing stronger state relations.

Important retained-velocity detail: `_spend_budget` can arrive and call `_settle(ARRIVED)`, which zeroes vx/vz, but `_advance_row` then writes the final displacement into vx/vz after `_spend_budget` returns. Thus ARRIVED may retain a nonzero final tick displacement and must not be uniformly zeroed/refused. ROUTE_LOST/PROFILE_STALE/CONTACT_STALE paths instead settle before that write or on a later height/transform refusal. IDLE stop resets velocity, remainders and targets. Public readers do not expose vx/vz, so a public probe can show final displacement/phase and stop-retained speed, while exact private bytes remain source-derived unless separately inspected and labeled.

Per-axis displacement is bounded by the released integer budget: at most ceil(speed/30), hence137 at maximum inherited speed4096. Each axis consumes at most its original tick budget across bounded route segments; losing unused budget at turns is an existing limitation, not a new movement policy. No legitimate public writer accepts arbitrary velocity. Arrival retains the last displacement, while phases3..5 should have zero velocity. A resident's pose may be moved by another public owner, but consumption still caps per-tick movement; exact route/pose/current profile association belongs to saved bindings.
