# Marlfox — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-MX-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

Performance is a working social institution. The Noonvale troupe needs provisions, has imperfect equipment, stages participatory entertainment and can become part of a host community. An audience includes adults who have finished their chores. The result feels like a lived settlement because time away from labor has recognizable people and purposes. [MX-D01, MX-D05, MX-D10]

Butty's dry-season record links aging, vacant leadership, fewer workers and thirsty orchards. A bucket chain makes the relationship between water access and the labor force visible. His voice combines complaint, affection and historical comparison; it is not a neutral telemetry report. Operational UI must still show actual water and staffing state. [MX-D02]

Cregga asks Dann to describe a home she has never seen. Her understanding of belonging comes through people, sound, touch and imagination. A blind elder is an authority and companion with a history, not a prop to make another character kind. Bells and familiar circulation are central to how home is recognized. [MX-D03]

## Places and movement

Dann's description places the path north–south beside a main gate; woodland borders the north and east and part of the south; a gatehouse lies inside; grounds contain pond, orchard, gardens and lawns around the tall Abbey with adjoining belltower. Use this as a composition reference with no invented distances. The source says bells mark dawn, midday, twilight and softly midnight in this era; other scenes use different calls. [MX-D03]

Sollertree's house combines stone slabs, timber, branches and mud chinking against a rock ledge. Sweet peat smoke, a tripod cauldron and a fruit tree carry memory and routine. This is a valuable intermediate style between freestanding cottage and deep excavation. [MX-D08]

The Swallow passage shows paddling pairs, branches used to hold a boat and a distinct launch. A wall-entry episode supplies a climb with a real anchor and window destination. These support explicit transitions, not free entry through every window. Tactical intrusion is outside the ordinary settlement movement adoption. [MX-D06–07]

## Additional direction

Preserve borrowing, gifts, keeping and stealing as different object histories in story metadata. Enable performance briefs to specify audience, preparation and aftermath. The island's later peaceful water-rat farming and Riverhead–Guosim boat learning are productive future diplomacy references; neither automatically changes the current admission or economy catalog.
