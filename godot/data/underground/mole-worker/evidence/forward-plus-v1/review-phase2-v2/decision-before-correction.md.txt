## Source and numerical contract

The finite source table records all65,536 actual native heading coefficient
pairs under the exact installed official Godot4.7.2 engine and actual Metal /
Forward Plus backend. It retains nonunit norms and small cardinal coefficients;
neither trigonometric idealization nor cardinal rounding substitutes for them.
The new wire/version and exact producer digest distinguish it from OpenGL-v1.
One active shared524,288-byte table uses the existing544,768-byte presentation
reservation, with bounded streaming metadata/row scratch. No simultaneous
second runtime table, per-resident copy or simulation-reserve borrowing is
authorized. Native allocator peak remains a measurement obligation.

Pin the exact engine RD skeleton compute shader, mesh packing and Forward
Clustered vertex/decode/model path, as well as the Metal shader compiler and
its actual options. The official Metal container uses fast math; the prior
GLSL4.10 highp guarantee cannot simply be copied. Derive a conservative bound
for the finite positive4/8-influence source expression, binary32 stores,
UNORM16 weights/compressed static vertices, common root and model arithmetic,
including permitted reassociation, contraction and subnormal behavior.
Unsupported arithmetic or unproved precision refuses rather than accepting
a guessed padding value. Existing source matrices/meshes may be reused only
as explicitly pinned mathematical inputs, with their historical producer
identity retained; they do not acquire a new renderer certificate by filename.

The first result may certify only a bounded source arithmetic component. A
rendered witness must separately exercise real native skin attachment/update,
mesh/material identity and cleanup on Metal. Sampled images or palette reads
alone do not prove continuous physical enclosure. No profile certificate bit,
World permission or current publication version changes in this phase.

wire version locally, bind v1 to its existing OpenGL backend and v2 to the
exact phase-one Metal/macOS/Forward+ source, and require the matching footer

second coefficient bank. The existing544,768-byte reservation does not grow.

component; complete Metal numerical enclosure and source-qualified profiles
