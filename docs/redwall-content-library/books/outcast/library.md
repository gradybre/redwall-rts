## Direction and interpretation

The strongest game foundation here is a warrior who wants to cultivate, cook and teach; a moving household that brings its stove and children along the river; and an Abbey whose generous ideals do not prevent serious mistakes. A settlement should feel full of particular relationships and ongoing work before its defenses are tested.

Keep Veil’s actions and others’ interpretations separate. He deliberately poisons, steals, assaults and traps his carers, then physically intervenes to protect Bryony. Meriam admits she was wrong to see no good in him; Bryony doubts his foresight. This disagreement belongs in narrative records. A species-to-morality stat would erase the book’s most useful tension and conflict with the project’s chosen foundation.

Sunflash’s power carries consequences: overextension, heat, sinking armor, loss of a scout friend, reopened wounds and prolonged grief. His later farmer-poet identity is explicit. At block3549 he changes his name to **Sunstripe**, resolving the apparent Salamandastron name discrepancy. His memorial remains private and sorrowful after prosperity returns.
