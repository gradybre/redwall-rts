`DESIGN-READ-TG-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

The frame explicitly treats history as collected testimony, practiced writing and a winter performance among friends. A recorder's ink-stained paws, an elder's slow walk and a warm room belong to the same world as adventurous deeds. The child's narration compares story ingredients to cooking while revealing family pride. An archive is a social workplace with audiences, apprentices and fallible perspectives. [TG-D01]

Cregga connects objects across generations: a carved wooden squirrel, misused as a doorstop, and a monocle retain relationships to Song, Janglur and Perigord. Their value is in remembered use and recognition, not an automatic stat effect. Mystery can begin with something ordinary that a skilled person identifies differently. [TG-D04–05]

Broggle's fine baking and needlework are both important. Fwirl's willingness to learn allows an intimate, ordinary teaching scene; this is especially useful for a colony whose residents should be more than job icons. Role, personal history and skill must remain separable. The broader Tagg/Sawney and Nimbalo material is in earlier TG-P01–04; it addresses belonging, refusal and private grief without requiring this pass to retell the plot.

## Movement and layout lessons

The Cavemob entrance is hidden behind a small waterfall; the stream continues into a cavern and central lake. Cooking sites occupy rock ledges and a family dining alcove is spatially distinct. Use the connected topology and work flow; its narrated thousands do not override the 256-resident settlement cap. [TG-D06]

The wall race has two nominated courses: parapet and adjacent grass, with separate starting arrangements for children. This supports route choice that respects individual comfort and ability, not a universal fear-of-heights trait for moles. It is a social-event reference, not approval for a racing minigame or dangerous child movement. [TG-D08]

The familiar door that will not stay open, a visible pond from an upper window and a cupboard's bottom shelf show how small physical facts support scenes. Observation from a window and reachability via stairs are different relationships. The accepted shared movement contract should preserve that distinction.

## Further opportunities

Write succession scenes around demonstrated care, learning and relationships. Use seasonal public readings as original presentation content. Keep emotional privacy separate from operational truth: character dialogue may defer a personal story, but death, need and job-state UI cannot conceal required facts. Research suggestions remain proposals until their owning systems specify costs and behavior.
