## Measured limitations

Every cast reverses underground by **π radians in one sampled update**. The
actual existing `turn_back` and `_walk_out` paths preserve position/progress,
but `stand_in_bore` immediately assigns the new travel heading. This is a
presentation-quality finding, not evidence that the route lost authority.

The log and radish are visible for 365 of 613 sampled carry-order frames for
mouse/mole/otter/badger, and 363 of 613 for squirrel. The rest are hidden under
the demo's current fade/turn/idle presentation lifecycle. The separate core
fixture deliberately retains its cargo for the whole capture, including after
the demo enters HOLD. This demonstrates the missing renderer-to-core lifecycle
binding; it does not prove that an already integrated real haul loses cargo.
UG09 must draw the actual carried reference across interruptions and reserve
its extent even while a presentation transition is incomplete. Hiding the load
cannot satisfy clearance.

Bounds here are global fixture-motion bounds, not root-relative production
body capsules. Tail/stoop and source blends are observed at finite times. The
logical-rig-to-mesh bridge, physical item variants, exact continuous/numerical
residual, complete recovery/contact/mode contract and profile revision remain
separate engineering work. Other crop props have the earlier static-state
observations; this batch does not claim all crop transition variants.
