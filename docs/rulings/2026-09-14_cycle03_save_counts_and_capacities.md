## SAVE-C3-R01 — Designated primary counts

`primary_count` is a **declared primary physical row extent for that owner block**, not a total of every field's extents and not an occupancy count. Every differently sized table retains its independent field `element_count` and validation. For a multi-table owner, name the primary table explicitly in its section contract. The section descriptor's `row_count` is **section-specific**: §4 and §5 keep their already-ruled checked sums of block primary counts; this ruling adopts §9's already-implemented designated-navigation value and fixes §8's one-block value. It does not establish a universal sum rule for all sections. [SAVE-LAYOUT-R01 §4/§5](2026-09-12_clock_restore_and_layout_followup.md#save-layout-r01); [§9 declared choice](../../godot/scripts/core/save_section_navigation.gd).

## REG-C3-R01 — Mechanical extraction with source proof

**Yes, mechanical numeric extraction may proceed, with row-level provenance and validation; no individual ruling is required for each unambiguous row.** This authorizes a *reviewable numeric sidecar/delta*, not blind replacement of the active registry or a runtime formula inferred from prose. The OPEN title's **501 of 590** is historical. On PR119's reconciled source, the registry is version **1**, **52** owners, **599** `hash:true` canonical records, **553** persisted packed-source fields; it also has **8** `hash:false` fields, hence **607** listed fields overall. The validator pins 599/553 and checks source membership. [registry header/census](../planning/canonical_state_registry.json); [PR119 validator](../validation/validate_save_registry_handoff.py).

Exactly **519 of 599** canonical records carry `shape.declared_capacity` as a string; **80** do not. All 519 match a bounded grammar `` `source expression` (=|<=) unsigned decimal ``: **473 exact `=`** and **46 upper-bound `<=`**, across **56 distinct expression/value strings**. The 46 bounds are 12 gear `_row_capacity <= 16384`, 12 inventory `_c_capacity <= 101376`, 14 inventory `_l_capacity <= 16384`, and 8 reservations `_row_capacity <= 32768`. A `<=` result means `max_capacity`, **not** exact `element_count`; the actual capacity/count must still come from and be validated against the owning store's persisted framing. For example, §8's five extents and §9's movement 512, request 8192, route descriptor 256, and spatial cell 262144 are exact declarations; §9's 15 remaining navigation fields have other shape rules and are outside this conversion. [§8 registry owner](../planning/canonical_state_registry.json); [§9 owners](../planning/canonical_state_registry.json); [bounded-capacity examples](../planning/canonical_state_registry.json).

For each of the **519** conversions, emit an audit row keyed by `(section_id, owner_key, ordinal, field_key)` with original prose, parsed expression, relation (`eq` or `lte`), numeric RHS, `source_module`, `source_member`, resolved defining constant/maximum and its source location or explicit dynamic-bound proof, source revision/hash, and verification status. Use a restricted parser/allowlisted arithmetic for products and qualified constants; never `eval`, pick an ambiguous symbol by name, or infer extent from the first column. Verify the numeric RHS against the owner code's actual constant or maximum and verify the field's wire extent/count rule separately. Quarantine missing, ambiguous, conflicting, overflowed, or unprovable rows for an explicit follow-up rather than silently converting them. Preserve 473/46 operator distinction; don't force variable stores to their maximum. Report every accepted conversion and every exception. [REG-R01 ordered/owner source rule](2026-09-12_save_registry_answers.md#reg-r01--ordered-registry-and-block-ownership).

**Version/field disposition.** Generate the numeric audit sidecar first while leaving `canonical_state_registry.json` v1 unchanged. A later reviewed registry edit may add validated numeric capacity metadata, with explicit schema/identity handling and deterministic artifact regeneration; do not silently change the registry JSON, claim unchanged source-registry SHA, or choose a new registry version number in this sidecar. Numeric metadata alone changes no save wire field, owner version, section version, canonical record count, or packed-source count. If the registry is made a production rules-manifest input, update/test its deterministic rules identity as [SAVE-R09-003](2026-09-11_save_codec_contract.md#save-r09-003--identity-producers) requires; do not assume a metadata-only edit leaves that identity unchanged. A later change to a field's *actual extent, type, order, persistence, or meaning* requires the normal owning version/compatibility decision under SAVE-R09-001.

**Acceptance for `#declared-capacity-as-prose`.** At this audited revision verify **599/553**, **519 = 473 exact + 46 bounds**, and **80 other canonical fields**, with no missing/duplicate audit keys. Recount and document later integrated changes; these are snapshot counts, not immutable format constants; assert the 8 non-hash rows were not swept in. Check all 56 distinct expressions against source definitions and the 519 row bindings; test equality, `<=`, qualified symbols, allowed products, ambiguous/missing symbols, bad arithmetic, and overflow. Include a valid below-maximum inventory/gear/reservation capacity fixture so bounds cannot accidentally become equality. Re-run registry/source handoff validation and a registry identity/field-order diff: expected canonical fields, ordinals, types, hash flags, and owner/section versions are unchanged. Exclude UI, movement gate, and Tier-2 economics decisions from this save-contract sidecar.
