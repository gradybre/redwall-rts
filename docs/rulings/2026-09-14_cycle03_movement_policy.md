## 1. Authored ford semantics — settle Q2-24 for the baseline ford

**Existing authority:** [GDD §5.1](../game_gdd.md) explicitly calls the natural ford walkable, at river exterior tiles x76–78, z48–51, y−128u; water surface is y0 and ordinary land y512u. The ford is not a fishing work tile. [WorldInit](../../godot/scripts/core/world_init.gd), `is_ford()`, `is_walkable()` and `elevation_y_units_of()`, implement that mask. [SpatialWorld](../../godot/scripts/core/spatial_world.gd), `is_ford_cell()`, already exposes it. [HAZ-001](../underground_economy_hazard_amendment.md) classifies ordinary ford walking with supported ground/tunnel walking, adding no hazard flag. PR123 §6.4 is right that Movement does not enforce a distinct mode predicate; it is wrong to treat the GDD's explicit walkability as merely geometry with no movement policy.

**Adopt the following implementation binding:**

1. `FORD_WALK` means supported walking across the explicitly authored ford, not swimming and not a generic “any sufficiently shallow water” predicate. Use the authoritative mask; do not require the water-column system to rediscover that existing classification. Its half-metre cells are x304–315 and z192–207 inclusive, derived by four cells per exterior tile. These are 192 marked cells, not 192 qualified body placements.
2. Derive the required mode from each actual route segment, not the caller's route-wide label. For the current adjacent-cell baseline, a segment with either endpoint on a ford cell requires `FORD_WALK`; an ordinary land-to-land segment requires `GROUND_WALK`. A mixed route must satisfy every required mode. Retain corner, clearance and continuous support/sweep checks; endpoint classification alone cannot legalize a diagonal through water or an oversized body extending beyond supported space. Expanded connections use their own declared type.
3. Keep the existing ground/ford speed and carry bindings and the baseline 10/14 route cost model. A ford label introduces neither a swim multiplier, oxygen charge, danger surcharge nor a new fixed entry toll. This preserves existing costs; it does not author new bank-transition durations. A future physical bank/step/shore connection must have its own geometry, supported sweep and any required timing before activation. The land-to-ford height difference is not an approved step-height capability, ramp or instantaneous vertical transfer.
4. The derived 128u difference between water surface and ford floor is **not** a universal per-species maximum water depth. New wading locations, changed water levels and generalized shore transitions remain separately authored. Other river/lake/coast water stays outside the baseline walking predicate. Never relabel unsupported water as ford to avoid a swim profile.
5. Do not demand dangerous-travel consent or HAZ-001's health70/rest4000 entry thresholds solely because an edge is a ford. Existing ordinary health, needs, load and profile rules still apply. Ford walking does not imply the occupant's airway is submerged; root Y alone cannot trigger diving air rules. Required body/support/airway compatibility remains a measured-profile gate. The wet ford is not automatically a dry floor-sleep or field-care landing: HAZ-005's supported recovery and dry-sleep requirements still apply.
6. Bind the current required segment mode and any mode transition to authoritative progress and save semantics. PR123 correctly observes that `_cursor_mode` is currently assigned at admission and subsequently exposed only as the admitted label. Do not silently reinterpret old saved route-wide labels as new per-edge state. Movement/G02 owns the explicit field/API/version change, if needed; no new binary encoding is assigned here.

**Effect:** Q2-24's baseline classification, inherited cost treatment and non-dangerous status are answered. Generalized wading depth and bank-transition geometry/timing remain open. Remove Q2-29's full water-column representation as a prerequisite for *classifying* the existing ford; retain actual fit/transition dependencies for production travel.

## 2. Child travel versus work — settle Q2-32 with an explicit action boundary

**The omitted higher-authority sentence:** [DEC-032](../setting_decisions.md#dec-032--adopted-dependent-resident-family-model), “Child activities”, says: **“No ordinary productive labor assignments, hazardous expeditions or excavation jobs.”** It also forbids helping animations secretly producing inventory or production XP. PR123 §2.3/Q2-32 narrowed that policy to hazardous *work* and excavation and missed hazardous expeditions. Its statement that non-work hazards are “not stated anywhere” must be corrected.

**Adopt this explicit engineering interpretation of that policy:** new deliberate CHILD travel through a HAZ-001 dangerous connection is prohibited, irrespective of whether the intention is work, needs, play/learning, a direct move, an expedition or accompanying an adult. Use HAZ-001's action classification: surface swimming, voluntary diving and **unprotected** climbing are dangerous. This maps the existing expedition exclusion to exact movement admission; it is not a claim that DEC-032 originally supplied those mode IDs or a new numerical child physiology rule.

- For each of the 16 species, CHILD voluntary `SWIM_SURFACE` and `DIVE` receive an explicit prohibited-action policy. Consent, a direct order, an escort's presence or a skill value cannot override it. A child also cannot be assigned an ordinary productive job even when its travel path is safe.
- `CLIMB` is conditional. Its unprotected case is prohibited for new deliberate child entry. A **qualified protected connection**, with HAZ-003's actual support/rescue arrangement and a compatible child profile, is eligible for nonproductive ordinary access. A rope icon, nearby adult or caller boolean cannot turn an unprotected connection into a protected one. Protected access to a platform does not authorize a child orchard-work job.
- Ground, the authored ford and completed supported tunnel access are not prohibited by child stage alone. Their actual CHILD profiles and PC-04 needs/care/routines remain required. No adult coefficients or geometry may be substituted. Child excavation remains prohibited even though properly supported dry excavation adds no HAZ danger flag.
- This is a **new-entry restriction**, not immunity, disappearance or a ban on recovering a child already in danger. Preserve occupancy, air, cargo and injury for an existing distressed occupant; execute only its validated committed return/recovery or an eligible rescue. A carried child is a patient in HAZ-004's checked relationship, not a self-swimming actor. Rescue needs the combined envelope, real support/airway and rescuer eligibility; it grants no child voluntary mode and cannot be used to schedule hazardous leisure travel. Children do not become productive rescue workers.

**Register consequence:** 32 CHILD species/mode rows have a definite prohibition on voluntary swim/dive. The 16 CHILD `CLIMB` rows need protected/unprotected predicates; do not mark all 48 rows wholly disabled. Retain six modes—protection is a connection property, not a seventh mode. Profiles and PC-04 remain not ready for the otherwise permissible child access. Replace `RC_CHILD_HAZ`'s unconditional grouping of the whole CLIMB mode, and correct `EV-LIFE-STAGE-COLUMN`'s unqualified “travelling as [a child] is not legal” to the actual current missing-profile refusal and these specific policies.

## 3. Ordinary access, elders and acquired ability — advance Q2-31/33/34

**Adopt a common ordinary-access policy for all catalog species:** badger, ferret, fox, hare, hedgehog, kestrel, mole, mouse, otter, rat, shrew, sparrow, squirrel, weasel, wildcat and wolverine. Supported ground, authored ford and completed dry supported tunnel walking are ordinary settlement access; none requires a species-specific innate permission or excavation skill. Qualified protected climbing infrastructure may likewise serve ordinary access without a species-exclusive veto. Body, stage, posture, grip, cargo, health and connection qualifications remain conjunctive requirements, not optional details.

This is a positive **policy permission subject to a complete profile**, not the existing catalog state `ENABLED`. It settles the policy portion of these access rows while their cost/measurement/implementation state can remain `AUTHORED_PROFILE_NOT_READY`. It does not grant universal natural-trunk climbing, swimming, diving, flight or access to every tunnel. [SET-MOVE-001 §§1–4](../movement_direction_amendment.md), MOVE-REQ-005/012/013 and [MOVE-DEP-R03](2026-09-12_movement_dependency_rulings.md) supply the ordinary connected-life, per-person and grounded-bird boundaries. Digging creates space; travelling through finished space is separate.

**Q2-33:** no ELDER-only mode veto, age penalty or stricter danger threshold is added in release 1. DEC-032 requires active individual elder roles and says age alone is not helplessness or a universal productivity penalty. Apply the same authored action policies and HAZ thresholds to an elder's actual state, with their explicitly authored stage profile and PC-04 rules. This answers the proposed blanket stage restriction; it does not make the elder physically identical to an adult or enable an unprofiled mode.

**Q2-34 — new bounded authoring choice:** this release's traversal eligibility is declared in versioned profiles and explicit connection/equipment/access conditions; it is not acquired by accumulating movement XP or raising an unrelated work skill. Do not add a learned-ability progression prerequisite to these six modes. Authored scenario/resident eligibility differences must have a real owned input and recorded provenance; no hidden mutable “trained swimmer” bit or new acquisition system is introduced here. This is an engineering choice for adoption within the existing optional training discussion, not a retroactive claim that anatomy or a book trained every resident. A later training progression requires a separate acquisition/state/save contract. PC-04's play/learning remains its own nonproductive routine scope.

## 5. Fix the register's distinction between prohibited and missing

PR123 §2.4 and `PROHIB-SITUATIONAL` put missing float/recovery/support definitions in one “already explicitly disabled” bucket. Preserve [MOVE-C2-R01's three meanings](2026-09-14_cycle02_movement_envelopes.md):

- A known prohibited action, such as deliberate child unprotected climbing, is **DISABLED** with its action context and source.
- A required missing profile, measured envelope, recovery definition or actual contact is **AUTHORED_PROFILE_NOT_READY**. Runtime entry still refuses, but lack of authoring is not an authored inability.
- A complete permitted profile can be **ENABLED** only under that meaning's existing completeness requirements; actual admission also checks current eligibility and qualified implementation. Occupied capacity can produce waiting; it is not a species restriction.

Do not replace the three catalog states with a fourth “policy-approved means ready” state. The planning register may separately record policy disposition, decision source and unresolved qualification fields; none of that is a new runtime enum. Protect the six existing mode IDs. Record action/protection variants beneath CLIMB instead of duplicating or discarding mode rows. Recompute 288 species/stage/mode leaves, report conditional child-climb policies separately, and keep production-qualified count zero until evidence changes it.

## 6. PR121 tooling — retain the method, repair uncovered error handling

The merged [validator](../../tools/validate_movement_envelopes.py), `quantize_axis_units()`, `_derive_class()` and `classes_admitting()`, implements Cycle 2's outward integer conversion and anchored lower-bound distinction; nonbaseline offsets remain report-only. [ADR0141](../decisions/0141-a-placement-refusal-is-not-a-clearance-class-refusal.md) and the [lane report](../tasks/lanes/05/2026-09-14-envelope-tooling.md) correctly disclaim model measurement/production qualification. Their CI-pending sentence is historical: master `.github/workflows/tests.yml`, line144, already runs `tools/test_movement_envelopes.py` after commit `a0e9c17`. The lane's reported124 checks and mutation results were not rerun in full in this sidecar.

**Reproduced accounting gap:** schema `measurement.interpolation_error_bound_units` is required but neither `record_semantic_problems()` nor `_quantized_local_bounds()` uses it to prove the supplied bounds/margins contain the declared error. In an entirely synthetic record with X/Z extrema ±250000 micrometres, baseline offset256, explicit zero margin/justification and interpolation error1u, schema/file/record validation returned no problems and `compute_fit()` returned `FIT_OK`, class1. Giving that same box a margin1u produces translated minima−1 and maxima513: `PLACEMENT_INCOMPATIBLE_AT_OFFSET`. This does not prove any real model is undersized; it proves the checker accepts a declaration whose error coverage is unestablished.

**Adopt explicit error accounting before accepting measured records:** define the error field as the residual conservative interpolation error not already enclosed by the submitted extrema. With the present scalar field, it bounds every axis. Require each axis margin to cover at least that residual error, plus whatever further allowance the owner declares. Zero residual is legal only with evidence that the bounds already enclose the full continuous sweep; then do not count the same error twice. A prose zero-margin justification cannot contradict a positive uncovered residual. Adopt movement envelope input schema **2** for this clarified residual-error meaning. Update validator, schema and fixtures together. Version1 records may be inspected as historical data but cannot qualify; their owner must reassert error coverage and source evidence in version2. This is an import-record version change, not a save owner/section version change. No numerical error or margin is supplied here.

**Protect the earlier import boundary too:** conversion from measured coordinates to PR121's signed integer micrometres must round minima down and maxima up, or carry a separately certified conservative residual allowance. Nearest/truncation at that earlier step is not repaired by later outward u-quantization. Synthetic upper extent250000.25µm requires257u; truncating it to250000µm produces256u and can falsely fit at the boundary. Negative minima have the corresponding failure. This is a measuring/export requirement; PR121 consumes integers and cannot reconstruct precision discarded before its input.

Schema-valid provenance strings are not authentication of a measured sweep. Qualification must bind actual source mesh/rig/clip/proportion and relevant gear variants to versioned hashes and the measured support datum; any procedural/static equivalent needs an explicit artifact, not omission. The current generic source hash and optional rig/clip names require that external qualification check or an expanded manifest. Preserve this as a measurement gate, not a ban on legal simulation when final rendering art is absent. PR121 `FIT_OK` stays an arithmetic result, not production approval.
