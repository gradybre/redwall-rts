## What this directory is

The proportion-comparison scene [decision 0002](../../../docs/decisions/) requires before
bulk creature proportions can be approved: mouse, mole, squirrel, otter and badger beside
the same doorway, table and work surface, standing, walking, carrying and crouching, at the
real RTS camera.

**It is a review input, not art.** The creature bodies are hand-authored box volumes — the
brief's step 1.2 blockout — at the ruling's **comparison-candidate** heights. Approving the
captures approves the proportions, not the modelling. **Every creature height here remains a
comparison candidate until Brendan approves it, and no bulk creature generation starts
before that.**

## Regenerating

```bash
# scene + manifest only. Works headless; captures are refused with a stated reason,
# because a --headless run uses Godot's dummy renderer and produces no image.
godot --headless --path godot --script assets/lookdev/build_proportion_comparison.gd

# scene + manifest + the six review captures. Needs a real rendering device.
godot --path godot --script assets/lookdev/build_proportion_comparison.gd
```

A capture that could not be rendered is **absent from the manifest and listed under
`capture_refusals` with its reason** — it is never listed as if the file existed.

## What the captures deliberately do not do

- They carry **no ART-LOCK-001 pigment, contour rule or light angle**. That lock governs UI
  illustration; the world follows the DEC-038 example, and the two are different treatments.
- They are untextured. A painted surface can hide wrong volume, which is exactly what this
  review is looking for.
- They establish **no performance result**. Triangle counts, draw surfaces and memory in
  `lookdev_dimensions.gd` are design ceilings, and GAP-10's Windows minimum-hardware
  qualification is untouched.
