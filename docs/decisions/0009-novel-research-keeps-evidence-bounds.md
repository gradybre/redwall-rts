# 0009 — Novel research keeps evidence and adaptation separate
Date: 2026-09-06 · Status: Accepted

## Decision
Keep the project research in `docs/redwall_novel_analysis.md` and link it from
the setting bible. Record actual reading coverage and distinguish primary
passages, publisher summaries, interpretation and proposed game applications.
This accepts a research-recording method, not its proposed creative additions.

## Why
The existing six-book guidance was based on publisher descriptions. Selected
excerpts now support closer analysis, but no complete focus novel has been
read for this pass. Treating those different evidence levels as interchangeable
would give later agents false confidence about canon and implementation scope.

## Consequences
- Follow SET-RESEARCH-001 Sections 2 and 7 when using its evidence.
- Its proposed applications do not close DEC records or authorize new systems.
- Brendan's adopted adaptations and content boundaries remain binding.
- Expand coverage only after reading the corresponding text; keyword searches
  and model recollection do not count as complete-novel reading.
- Existing mechanical owners retain numeric authority.
- This task leaves the research and companion updates uncommitted for review,
  under the user's review-before-commit workflow; decision 0007's general
  commit direction does not override that instruction.
