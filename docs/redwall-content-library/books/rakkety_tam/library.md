# Rakkety Tam — systematic content library

## Scope and evidence

All 583 normalized PDF blocks were read sequentially in 18 untruncated chunks, including the prologue, all three parts, 40 numbered chapters, epilogue and end matter. Every reading interval has durable extraction notes and a `FULLY_INSPECTED` ledger entry. Exact PDF source-unit and block locators appear in `catalog.json`; `coverage.json` records the coverage audit. This is a full reading and systematic editorial extraction, not a claim of infallible enumeration.

The catalog separates narrated events, character reports, jokes and interpretive beliefs. Recipe records separately preserve source ingredients, explicit preparation, occurrence status, proposed game ingredients and an `AI_AUTHORED_GAME_ADAPTATION` method. A food in a song, a pretend meal, a rejected apple or an unfulfilled breakfast promise is not silently presented as an observed serving. No full passages or lyrics are reproduced.

## Direction dossier

### An inhabited refuge, maintained through work

Redwall's warmth comes from visible activities: Humble still lives near his old cellar trade, Burlop brews and cooperages, Demple staggers planting against store capacity, and kitchen helpers move food between gardens, ovens, cellars, trestles and diners. The festival honors the moles who built underground facilities. Everyday labor should create spatial activity, recognizable tools and interpersonal memory rather than merely an income number. The first person met at a workbench can become a host, witness, worried parent, defender or grieving friend. [Blocks18–25,60–81,135–138,261–277]

Burlop is the clearest corrective to permanent combat classes. He willingly carries his trade tools to defend the Abbey, is devastated by killing, and subsequently contributes through brewing, care and warm food for the watch. His value does not diminish because he stops fighting. Tam likewise ends the book as husband and father in a settled home. Statements that warriors are born to fight are character philosophy, contradicted by these lives; they must not become inherited species or class restrictions. [Blocks256,334,379–384,490,546,570–579]

### Food is technology, hospitality and identity

Didjety's sausages are unusually complete source food technology: finely ground barley and oats mixed with carrot and mushroom, wrapped in onion skin and baked slowly overnight. Her pastry-wrapped rolls later spread to Redwall because she shares the recipe with Glisum. This is direct support for recipe learning through visits and relationships. Her forager soup combines toasted oatcake crumbs, water, garlic, mushrooms, greens, roots, petals and carried rock salt; source botanical safety remains fictional, and the game counterpart uses explicit substitutions where needed. [Blocks430,456,518–526,571]

Burlop's fruit-honey concentrate reduces the carried liquid while still requiring local water. Its quarter-beaker serving topped with water gives the inherited ratio `concentrate : water = 1 : 3`. That is source preparation, not an originated production yield. The river cooks' troutbake provides another unusually explicit process: fish on hot stones under apple, celery, onion and watercress, then a covered pit. Preserve ingredient and process knowledge while leaving exact work ticks, station dimensions, fuel costs and yields to the shared balance specification. [Blocks196–202,356–359]

Feasts use menus, serving architecture and social differences. Front-step buffets differ from progressive Great Hall courses and boat-awning snacks. Cooks plan for hare appetites, but grief can quiet diners without ending hunger. Hot farls and cordial reach guards on cold ramparts; a shared cheese-onion farl advances a relationship. [Blocks261–277,469–470,494–500,546–548]

### Movement has places, costs and teachers

Canopy movement is a sequence of connected trees, ledges and exits. Tam and Doogy use a hornbeam to reach the captive oak, and the escape has a named branch route. Armel explicitly lacks this skill despite being a squirrel and improves with instruction. A tree court, a bank burrow and a root-complex home are separate settlement forms with different entrance and occupancy needs. Wall-base tunnel reconnaissance is explicitly mentioned by enemy scouts; it belongs in fortification threat modeling even though this novel does not narrate a completed siege tunnel. [Blocks44,149–150,240–247,313,329,412–413]

Water is both infrastructure and danger. Skipper swims unseen to inspect a bank hole, later retrieves a cached sword underwater, and is himself hauled into a boat after an ambush. A swift current carries an exhausted Tam beyond his control; rescuers need line of sight and a reach tool. Boats outrun bank travelers but can be trapped by a fallen tree, miss an inlet or enter a waterfall. The Guosim's bold grapnel-assisted turn is explicitly a first attempt. Their earlier tree-removal effort kills workers despite clearing the route. Those failures must remain part of the systems reference. [Blocks280–281,436–464,486]

### Voices, humor and grief

Keep the Borderer's direct warmth, Doogy's Highland stubbornness, the voles' teasing exaggeration, mole practicality, military ritual and Abbey plain speech distinguishable with light dialect. Animal anatomy should communicate feeling: Doogy hides his embarrassment behind his tail, otters gesture with rudders, birds' wings and talons affect posture and tool use. Songs belong to work, bedtime, marching, mockery and homecoming; their function matters more than reproducing lyrics. [Blocks294–300,347–355,437–443,520–534]

Named deaths leave personal objects and changed relationships. Dauncey's dispatch purse and belt mark his grave; his nephew later carries his name. Crumshaw's bier lies below the tapestry and his loss deepens Tergen's isolation. Butty's last stand and the shrews' work accident are remembered before a meal. Humor resumes without proving grief is finished. [Blocks258–260,398–403,450–452,470,500,573]

### Leadership, enemies and uncertain faith

The heroes are not morally uniform. Their source conduct includes no-quarter declarations, coercive interrogations, humiliation and physical force against distressed or injured allies. Preserve those contradictions in the reference while keeping the user's exclusions: no torture mechanics and no cruelty toward children. Armel's discomfort and Burlop's remorse are equally canonical and should guide humane adaptations. [Blocks112,132,242–246,327,379–384,402,429–432,542–555,573]

Gulo's followers act under fear; useful reports are suppressed, witnesses are killed before questions are answered and convenient lies multiply. Freeta supplies real planning intelligence; the species of a commander does not substitute for motives or competence. Squirrel royalty also oppresses, and the ending refuses a new heroic monarchy. [Blocks167,283–285,329–330,444,505–510,573]

Shared dreams, remembered verses and Tam's subjective vision shape choices without requiring the game to certify supernatural facts. Rockbottom's revelation grounds an apparently mystical political relic in a living, cared-for tortoise. Do not turn him into ore, inventory currency or a harvest animal. [Blocks430–431,487–499,539–555]
