## Game direction

This book supplies four complementary community forms: cultivated Abbey, military mountain, river-colony network and secluded island household. Their ordinary lives need different architecture, foods, work habits and voices. Connect them through hospitality, rescue and mutual obligation; combat does not replace their civilian identity.

Mara’s movement from mountain to Abbey is a choice shaped by affection, grief and a desire for a different life. Her role changes without changing her species. After the final battle the survivors feel weary and ashamed, and Pennybright asks for sunlight. This should guide recovery scenes, quiet animation, supportive companionship and relocation choices.

Warmth comes through a working kitchen, a cellarkeeper’s friendship, practical gifts, borrowed accents, elderly quiltmaking, children’s play and shared meals. The final banquet offers unusually detailed direct food and spatial reference. Dumble’s much later waiting for bird friends keeps the ending affectionate and bittersweet.
