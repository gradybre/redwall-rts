Date: 2026-10-03. Author: independent Construction lane.

This is a read-only assessment of decision 1101, the current actual owner APIs,
and the source motion bounds below. It covers L0, four treads, and L1. It is not
a route, motion, support, material-contact, or playable certificate. No game
source, geometry, recipe, profile flag, or price was changed for this assessment.

The current downward work descriptor alone does **not** establish an executable
cut/install sequence. A paid, finished lateral work bench is a plausible way
to preserve access during the lower cuts, but it still needs explicit immutable
geometry, actual access and retreat, bearing dependencies, and installation
contacts. The missing facts cannot be replaced by a completed-prefix integer.

## What the current sequence cannot assume

1. **A 512 u tread is not a 647 u workstation.** The deficit is 135 u. Adjacent
   treads are at different heights and cannot be combined into a fictional
   flat stance. Top-face contact at local Y = 0 also requires root Y to equal
   the actual target cube's top. Roots 256, 0, -256 do not match the 1024 u
   lattice with datum Y = 512.
2. **L0 requires its own paid pocket.** A positive-thickness deck whose top
   is 512 extends into original ground. The illustrative root at s = 1664 ahead
   of the first stair-run cube lies inside L0's eventual cut footprint.
   It cannot be a protected natural anchor outside the complete EntryPlan.
   It is available later only through actual installed L0 support.
3. **T2/T3 share physical keys in the original alignment.** Both occupy
   s[3072,4096). T3's positive thickness below top -512 needs cuts from the
   lower course Y[-1536,-512], in addition to the upper course [-512,512].
   A lower cut must not remove an installed T2 bearer or its retained-earth
   bearing. Either those dependencies are proved independent, the relevant
   cut happens first from another valid station, or an explicitly authored
   placement phase separates the dependencies. No installed part can simply
   be ignored by excavation.
4. **L1 cannot be its own final work station.** Its positive-thickness timber
   needs the lower course under its footprint removed. The last such cut
   removes the natural floor that an uninstalled L1 might otherwise offer.
   A worker, input container, output container, or retreat route cannot keep
   relying on that floor until the whole landing installs.
5. **CUT is insufficient for a bench route.** OPEN_UNFINISHED is not public
   access (ECON-003). A used natural bench needs paid BRACE/CUT/FINISH,
   actual retained footing, its own truthful datum/contact, and completed
   incoming and retreat edges. Ordinary 1093 flat-room roof/footing bands
   cannot protect the entrance cap or a bench floor that the next entry cut
   intentionally removes.

## Conditional paid bench sequence

The following ordering is a content/frontier target for validation, not a
claim that the current source already executes it:

1. Prove external natural anchors outside **all** planned physical cuts,
   including L0, work benches, and timber/bearing pockets. Deliver bracing
   inputs and provide actual local earth output before each phase starts.
2. Pay the L0 pockets from those external contacts; install the entire L0
   assembly only from an actual external installation contact. Its included
   bearers must have real support. No route uses its future deck beforehand.
3. Remove the required upper course from completed L0/exterior surface
   contacts. For later longitudinal cells, an exterior side station may be
   needed: the worker cannot use a fractional tread as a top-face root.
   Install the next complete tread assemblies only when their actual input,
   work and support dependencies pass. A compact installation/entry pose is
   still needed; the old 647 u work union does not grant it.
4. Reach a paid, finished bench at Y=-512 through a certified installed
   prefix/natural step. A 256 u step down from T2 is geometrically suggested,
   but requires an actual edge and whole motion/retreat proof. Preserve an
   outside lateral footing through all lower T3/L1 cuts and landing assembly
   work. Do not charge this earth twice or invent a scaffold.
5. Complete the lower cuts without removing the station, prior-part bearing,
   material/output endpoint, or sole retreat. Install T3 and the whole L1
   in the accepted assembly order. Publish supported endpoints only when both
   paid physical completion and installed timber/support qualify. Before
   withdrawing a temporary bench contact, protect a different completed route
   and relocate occupants and inventory. Retained physical bench volume either
   remains part of the Corridor or undergoes actual paid closure/backfill;
   deleting its designation does not restore dirt.

## Top Y = 512 versus 1536

Y = 512 already lies on the economic datum. Datum alignment alone cannot prevent
its positive-thickness landing from intersecting ground. Raising the entire
unchanged variant to 1536 can keep a sufficiently thin L0 deck above ground,
but only if its actual supports/footings and access also validate. It creates
an initial 1 m climb from the real surface and does not create free posts or
a free approach. Surface LevelCatalog level 0 explicitly accepts offset 0 only.

The same 20 risers then end at -3584 instead of-4608. This is a different endpoint
placement; choosing an underground section offset would change the actual
section/roof clearance and still require all current source proofs. Keeping
the original bottom instead requires a different descent geometry. Raising
only L0 while preserving T0 at 256 instead creates a 1280 u first drop. None of
these changes is adopted here. Fractional tread heights and the 647-versus-512
stance problem remain even after the whole-metre translation.

## Minimal existing-owner seam for a distinct EntryPlan

The flat RoomPlan in `underground_room_orders.gd:113` and its current cold
admission remain unchanged. Its single origin/height and the flat CutMap input
cannot represent this 3D entry by stretching a bounding rectangle. Calling
confirm_room repeatedly would create partial/multiple Room ownership rather
than one permanent Corridor.

Use a separate typed EntryRequest/EntryPlan over actual pinned Catalog,
Assemblies, Recipes and the future physical-frontier source. It supplies the
exact transform, original Space revision, full initial anchor, opening targets,
and source-derived varying-height claim/cut prisms. Assembly grouping's existing
frontier hash is a **billable grouping pin**, not this missing physical frontier.
The latter must name complete before/after work contacts, support/bearing
dependencies, paid cut sets, install contacts, material/output contacts and
legal retreat. Caller success booleans or arbitrary cursor implementations
must not authorize any of them.

RoomOrders needs a separate confirmation entry point under its existing exclusive
admission stage and same actual World Budget. Before any provider callback it
pins the selected request kind; after lease admission it pins the complete entry
input/source tuple. Its existing pure candidate/scope/companion/publication
readers must compare that retained entry rather than a dummy flat RoomPlan.
Room type is permanently CORRIDOR. Both paths can share the actual Directory
candidate, Buildings registration, final publication order and cleanup.

For the entry path, replace only the flat geometry builder with one metadata
FLOOR_DATUM identifying the entry excavation section and exact 3D
OBSTACLE+CLAIM_ROOM boxes. These are reservations, never completed air/support
or a usable stair. Existing future-Room Space publication already permits these
role forms. Later installed tread/landing datum rows supply their real root Y;
the initial section envelope must never stand in for those walking floors.

After all companion survey images are sealed/dropped, prepare a distinct typed
Sites EntryClaimInput through the proposed concrete union-of-boxes cursor.
Root has leased this cohesive 1107 increment. The cursor uses the same immutable
Domain and unique Y/Z/X physical key order, with bounded work and prepaid replay.
Sites pins the private input and retains its existing final count/history/full
identity guard, SOLID rows and sorted merge. No second all-cut-key list, paid
ledger, per-quantum metadata columns or cursor callback appears after identity
allocation. Its incremental scratch is 24 × B bytes of private boxes, where B is the box
count, plus the concrete cursor interval bank; every other input image and companion lifetime is counted
by the caller before allocation.

Preserve the exact last boundary already in RoomOrders: **all observers,
current source/lease/request proofs, then the callback-free Sites guard;
Directory/Buildings Room allocation; Sites keys; sealed Space; prepared
companions.** A separate later fallible Placement.register cannot silently
be treated as part of atomic confirmation. The admission must preflight and
prepare actual Placement capacity/identity or explicitly retain an accepted,
unstarted Corridor for a retry; it cannot claim a refused order rolled back a
Room that already exists.

The current flat actual binding also requires one catalogued roof/floor band
(`underground_room_bindings.gd:264`). Entry admission therefore needs its own
actual source/structure proof within the sole bound RoomOrders authority. Do
not weaken the flat validator or rebind Buildings to a replacement authority.
The first WorkFace observation needs no fake Room/Job; productive START/WORK
later requires the actual assigned worker, Gear, load, material/output and
retreat facts. Missing content remains an explicit refusal.
