## Exact source boundary

Use the unchanged reviewed grip helper to reconstruct its exact derivative
from the original imported mole and pick. The unchanged ActorContent loader
must match those meshes to the exact accepted immutable fourteen-clip image.
Read each real RenderingServer surface on Metal/Forward+ and retain its full
vertex, attribute, skin and index buffer bytes, format, counts, strides,
offsets and AABB. The pinned engine's MeshStorage reader gets these from the
actual RenderingDevice buffers. Also retain the actual native decoded arrays
used by the existing finite palette proof. Every original surface, vertex,
influence and index must be accounted for. Source/import hashes are recorded
before and after the isolated capture; drift or unavailable input refuses.

The Python reader checks a finite complete binary image and exact footer/hash.
It derives offsets independently from the pinned engine layout, compares all
bone IDs and integer weight words with the decoded reference, and retains
the exact rational UNORM values. Compressed static positions are interpreted
through their actual AABB and separately compared with the source reference.
No decoded weight is silently renormalized. Unsupported skin compression,
blend shapes, custom vertex fields, layouts or primitive modes refuse.

The result must distinguish exact packing/CPU-decode facts from the still
unproved Metal built-in conversion and optimized shader arithmetic. Reading
GPU input storage does not read the deformed output or certify a motion.

## Bounds and ownership

Only the new capture, its focused Godot test, new Python auditor/test and
`forward-plus-v1/rd-input-v1/` are owned here. Existing Actor/Profile/content
and phase-three audit sources stay unchanged. The first admission is exactly
two primary source parts (firm-grip body plus original pick), plus at most one
explicit shadow mesh per primary part. Recursive shadow meshes refuse. Each
mesh has at most eight surfaces and 32,768 total vertices, each surface at
most 196,608 indices and eight separately retained LOD index streams, 4/8 skin influences,
64 bones, and at most16MiB raw/reference data per surface. A complete capture
is capped at64MiB. Native capture and offline audit process one surface at a
time; raw/decode/reference coexistence is recorded separately. Exact original
asset/source identity bounds the loaded native resources; their native peak
is measured as a diagnostic, not asserted from packed-byte arithmetic.

These are offline witness limits, not a new gameplay or whole-client budget.
No simulation/presentation reserve or qualification flag changes. Tests must
reject omitted/duplicate surfaces, mismatched source/derivative identity,
wrong bone/weight words, bad strides/counts, truncation/trailing bytes and
source drift. Actual Metal evidence must name the backend, engine and full
input census, and preserve any refused iterations.
