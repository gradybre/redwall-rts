# Settlement Economy: Catalogs, Labor, and Validation

Specification `SET-BALANCE-001`, revision 1.1; authored 2026-09-05. Scope: settlement release 1. This is an implementation contract and an analytical audit, **not a certified three-year playthrough**. Read §8 and Conflicts Found before treating a calculated surplus as demonstrated survival.

Ruleset v2 follows the adopted [setting_rules_amendment.md](setting_rules_amendment.md). This revision removes land-animal hunting and uses scenario-bound admission. Changed content is tagged `[AMEND-001]`; unchanged numeric values remain inherited.

## 1. Authority and arithmetic

**BAL-AUTH-001.** The system SHALL preserve every numeric rule in `game_gdd.md`. `[GDD §x]` identifies inherited values; `[NEW]` identifies a decision made here; `[DERIVED]` identifies arithmetic using the cited operands, not a replacement balance value. These annotations apply to every value in the associated row or paragraph unless a field-specific annotation overrides them. GDD requirement IDs remain authoritative. A conflict here does not authorize editing the GDD.

**BAL-AUTH-002.** The implementation SHALL use `floor_div(a,b)` for a nonnegative quotient, `ceil_div(a,b)=(a+b-1)//b`, and `trunc_div(a,b)=sign(a)*floor_div(abs(a),b)` for signed quotients; denominators SHALL be positive. `//` throughout this document means floor division on nonnegative operands. Signed operations explicitly say `trunc_div`. Fractions in ratio columns are exact numerator/denominator pairs, not executable implicit division. No intermediate rounding is allowed unless stated. `[GDD §4.1; NEW notation]`

**BAL-AUTH-003.** Quantities use signed 64-bit milli-U, work uses signed 64-bit milli-WU, and position uses signed 32-bit units of 1/1024 m. One U is 1000 milli-U and one WU is 1000 milli-WU. Need and mood ranges are 0–10000; health is 0–100. The living cap is 256; the resident slot capacity is 512. `[GDD §4.1–4.2]`

**BAL-TIME-001.** At completed tick `t`, calculate `absolute_hour=floor_div(t+4500,750)`, `absolute_day=1+floor_div(t+4500,18000)`, `hour=absolute_hour mod 24`, `season=floor_div(absolute_day-1,12) mod 4`, `local_day=1+(absolute_day-1) mod 12`, and `year=1+floor_div(absolute_day-1,48)`. Initialization is day 1 at 06:00; first midnight is tick 13500. The end of day 144 is tick 2587500; three complete elapsed years from initialization end at tick 2592000, day 145 at 06:00. `[DERIVED: GDD §5.1]`

**BAL-NUM-001.** When integrating a rational rate, the system SHALL retain its remainder across ticks, task switches, saves, and worker changes. At an upper/lower need bound discard only the remainder that would push farther outside the bound. Nutrition, yield, and material discounts round down; required trips, whole portions, batches, beds, and stations round up. Inventory capacity debits use `ceil_div(quantity_milli*mass_g,1000)` per lot, so splitting cannot create free carrying capacity. `[GDD §5.2, §5.8; NEW conservative mass rounding]`

*Rationale: integer storage alone is insufficient unless every conversion and retained fraction is specified.*

## 2. Catalog compilation contract

**BAL-CAT-001.** Tables in §3–4 populate the four required definition types. IDs are case-sensitive ASCII `StringName` keys. Preserve recipe and item IDs explicitly written in the GDD. Where the GDD gives only a display label, the new mapping is identified as `[NEW key]`; do not claim that the label was an existing ID. Compile each domain independently in ascending ASCII key order, preserving explicitly numbered gameplay enums. Record the resulting mapping in `catalog_ids.json` and verify its hash on load. `[GDD §4.3]`

**BAL-CAT-002.** Category, effect, recipe-family, crop-family, and station domains use the keys printed in these tables and numeric IDs generated by BAL-CAT-001. Unlock values are M0=0, M1=1, M2=2, M3=3, M4=4 `[NEW encoding of GDD §5.11 milestones]`. Booleans are numeric 0 or 1. `shelf_hours=0` means unlimited. Every recipe quantity is milli-U and every work value is milli-WU. Lists in an input/output column are typed pair lists, not numeric columns. `[GDD §4.3, §5.7–5.8]`

**BAL-CAT-003.** Item `effect` and `effect_value` describe a catalog ingredient's full-strength effect. Effects on prepared output are derived from the consumed input snapshot; `FROM_INPUT` with catalog value 0 indicates this runtime derivation, not absence of an effect. Duration is 4500 ticks for the ingredient effects in §5.7. For raw consumption halve magnitude using signed truncation. Satiety=-50 per 1000 hunger decay; STAMINA=-50 per 1000 awake rest decay; WARMTH=-250 per 1000 exposure; RECOVERY=1 health/hour; CHEER=200 mood; PURPOSE=100 per 1000 restoration; SOCIAL=100 per 1000 restoration. `[GDD §5.7; NEW symbolic keys and signed encoding]`

**BAL-CAT-004.** `fish` is an input-category selector over trout, dace, salmon, perch, carp, whitefish, herring, mackerel, mussel. It is not a tenth fish stock or an item that can be spawned. For each reservation choose legal concrete lots using GDD expiry ordering, then keep that binding through completion. Sapient eels and pike encounters are excluded. Compost's alternative input sets are mutually exclusive complete 4000-milli-U sets; do not combine roots and spoiled_food unless a later rules revision adds that recipe. Salt's source field is explicitly `brine`, retaining the GDD's coastal-only restriction on its displayed water ingredient. `[GDD §5.4, §5.7; NEW alternative-set selection: spoiled_food first, roots second]`

**BAL-CAT-005.** Recipe family is FOOD for meals/rations, PRESERVED for edible preservation, MATERIAL for ordinary crafting, GEAR for equipment, PROPAGATION for seeds/saplings, ECOLOGY for compost/recolonization, EXTRACTION for source jobs, CARE for treatment, and SERVICE for maintenance `[NEW]`. Family labels never grant bonuses or override GDD skill indices. Only the 24 active recipe-table entries and explicitly named ancillary conversions are compiled; basic movement, socializing, and sleep are actions, not material recipes. `[GDD §5.3–5.9]`

**BAL-CAT-006.** Complete building definitions by joining each base row with its explicitly keyed upgrade and furniture rows. Do not invent a tier-2 mill. Only residence, hall, covered_store, and workshop accept the GDD tier-2 packages; interpreting the generic word “store” as covered_store is a `[NEW]` restriction flagged in Conflicts Found. A shelf adds its 50000 g to the pantry service, not to an unrelated building's industrial buffer. Station worker slots and passive batch slots are distinct. `[GDD §5.9, REQ-SET-136]`

**BAL-CAT-007.** A building ID denotes its exterior structure. Furniture definitions occupy a separate domain; they use the same cost/work fields but do not count as exterior structures. Nonmanaged buildings have `room_tiles=0`. An exterior building with no operational inventory need has a main container of zero capacity until an explicit service needs it; production stations use the exact buffers in §4.2 below. `[GDD §4.2, §5.9; NEW zero-capacity inactive container policy]`

**BAL-CAT-011.** Recipe `station` indexes a **service domain**, not the BuildingDefinition index. Keys are brewery, composter, dryer, kitchen, mill, nursery, preserver, saltpan, well, workbench, workshop `[NEW domain]`. A kitchen service is supplied by either one exterior kitchen's 2 cooking slots or each valid interior kitchen_bench's 1 slot. Thus the starter hall can cook at its bench without first constructing an exterior kitchen. An order's building reference is the owning exterior building; the assigned job destination is the exact eligible bench or exterior station. `[GDD §5.7, §5.9; NEW service binding]`

## 5. Work and labor accounting

**BAL-WORK-001.** Calculate `skill_factor=1000+50*L`. Mood factors are 600 below 2000, 800 from 2000–3999, 1000 from 4000–6999, 1100 from 7000–8499, and 1150 from 8500–10000. Health factors are 600 below 40, 850 from 40–69, and 1000 from 70–100. Set `F=clamp(floor_div(skill_factor*mood_factor*health_factor,1000000),300,1800)`. Add `80*F` to a work remainder each productive tick; transfer `floor_div(remainder,1000)` milli-WU to job progress, retaining the residue. Apply weather, darkness, and workshop factors as additional rational factors with one final floor and retained remainder. `[GDD §5.2–5.3, §5.9–5.10]`

**BAL-WORK-002.** Recipe work is invariant when skill improves. Higher skill reduces elapsed base-time opportunity cost; it does not change a recipe's declared WU. For a job of `W` milli-WU with constant F, no additional modifier, and initial work remainder 0, required ticks are `ceil_div(W*1000,80*F)`. The labor tables report base-time milli-WU occupied, `80*ticks`, so productive work, waiting, and walking can share a unit. XP is credited from completed productive work, not this opportunity cost. Passive processing occupies the station for its exact time and consumes zero worker WU. `[GDD §5.3, §5.7; NEW table reporting convention]`

**BAL-WORK-003.** For a path with quantized leg length `D` units and constant movement cap `v`, travel ticks are `ceil_div(D*30,v)` for a straight legal leg from rest with instantaneous cap travel. For `Q` milli-U at mass `M` grams/U, trips=`ceil_div(ceil_div(Q*M,1000),carry_g-other_cargo_g)`. Split mixed-item cargo using per-lot ceiling mass; each actual departure records exact payload.
