# Theme, feeling and material direction for the game

This synthesis connects the fully inspected supplied narratives to the user's accepted creative direction. It is interpretive game direction, supported by the linked per-book catalogs; it is not a claim that every novel shares one uncomplicated moral or aesthetic rule. The full character, faction, food, ingredient, object, ecology, custom and system directories remain the content library.

## 1. The community is the reason to play

Make the player recognize what a community does when nobody is attacking it. Meals have cooks and servers; cellars have particular keepers; young residents ask questions; elders teach; performers arrive; tired travelers need beds; buildings accumulate repairs and memories. Defense matters because these routines and people already matter.

This is visible in Abbey domestic life across the corpus, Noonvale's productive landscape, mole and hedgehog homes, family flotillas, sea-otter households and improvised refuges. Lord Brocktree's supply cave becomes a lived-in shelter with space, ovens and bedding. Outcast assigns work in response to preference and ability: an unhappy cook can move into brewing while another resident takes the kitchen. Rakkety Tam adds staggered planting and food preservation to this productive world. These references support ordinary life as playable substance.

| Domain | Preserve from the references | Concrete authoring consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food | Named makers, household specialties, preparation texture, seasonal stores, hospitality and memory | Assign selected dishes to occasions, workspaces and makers; show chopping boards, bowls, shelves, pans and serving actions |
| Home | Distinct dwelling forms, reused rooms, sheltered corners, bedding, repair and accumulated possessions | Each inhabited form gets sleeping, storage, access and communal use rather than a decorative shell |
| Work | Practical knowledge, preferred roles, apprenticeship, communal effort and improvisation | Write task briefs around what is produced and who is helped; do not make all noncombatants interchangeable |
| Leadership | Service, judgment, legitimacy, error, listening and succession | Distinguish office, experience, personality and political institution; show the consequences of poor orders |
| Belonging | Refuge, adoption, voluntary alliance, return home, outsider trust and contested prejudice | Preserve individual history and chosen ties under the accepted admission policy |
| Memory | Stories, archives, graves, artifacts, inherited names and honored service | Use visible room/object changes and event references; no automatic resurrection or amnesia after loss |

## 3. Art direction: recognizable creatures in a lived world

The accepted visual blend combines storybook warmth with grounded materials and recognizable animal anatomy. The reviewed screenshots remain direct modeling references in `docs/art-reference/model_reference_guide.md`; the novel library contributes clothing, tools, food, room use, wear and character-specific details. A literary label alone cannot supply missing front, side or rear model views.

| Asset family | Source-supported detail to select | Required authored decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residents | Species anatomy, individual clothing or carried items where stated, age and injury, occupations and habits | Approved project proportions, mesh topology, rig, views, camera readability and actual palette |
| Kitchen props | Griddle stones, shell bowls, earthenware, cauldrons, ladles, flagons, pastry dishes, knives and baskets | Which props match each selected recipe and workstation; dimensions and collision remain project-authored |
| Food | Grain texture, crust, fruit/nut pieces, glazes, cream layers, soups and cellar drinks | Selected game ingredient variant, portion presentation, mesh/texture budget and serving container |
| Earth-built homes | Roots, cavities, timber, hearths, storage and domestic chambers | Construction method, load-bearing visual logic, entrances, drainage cues and connected interior space |
| Fortified communities | Worn masonry, halls, stairs, windows, water stores, forge, repairs and occupation damage | Era-specific state, navigable plan, scale, destruction scope and supported gameplay cues |
| Travel and water | Boats, ropes, sailcloth, paddles, packed provisions, repair sites and landing places | Approved craft role, passenger/cargo handling, rig limits and route compatibility |
| Memory and service | Graves, portraits, medals, inherited shawls, weapons, repaired clothing and recorded stories | Selected event provenance, ownership, respectful presentation and persistence scope |

A visual brief must connect each significant object to an intended use or story association. A room's scattered tools should correspond to plausible work. A retired or disabled character can retain visual dignity, recognizable tools and an active role. Neither injury nor age should be reduced to a comic silhouette.

## 4. Food as culture and economy

The books contain more than banquets. The library distinguishes breakfasts, field rations, recovery meals, desired foods during hunger, remembered household specialties, children's treats, formal gifts, daily beverages, harvest stores, comic songs and coercive eating contexts. The same dish can change meaning by occasion.

Use dishes to make production chains emotionally legible: a stored fruit can become a preserve, travel concentrate, shared tart or a welcome drink. Different makers and regions can use distinct variants without requiring hundreds of redundant resource types. The eventual runtime catalog must select a manageable approved subset under the owning economy specification; the research index remains broad.

Do not copy every source animal ingredient into the game. Do not rewrite the books to pretend those ingredients were absent. Do not assume every cheese is dairy or every milk is greensap. Outcast's particular greensap preparation is useful evidence precisely because it is explicit and local. The pantry's oat and seed completions are labeled game choices.

The source songs sometimes give actual preparation information, such as the Long Patrol brewing ingredients, and sometimes give impossible comic food. Preserve the distinction. Copying lyrics is unnecessary: the design value lies in occasion, rhythm, social role, named ingredients and narrative function.

## 5. Voice, humor, music and sensory feel

Distinct voices should come through vocabulary, sentence rhythm, confidence, patience, habits, professional knowledge and relationships. Light dialect is the user's selected direction. Source phonetic spelling can identify a style reference, but dense transcription should not become a readability barrier or a sign of lesser intelligence.

| Situation | Selected tone | Concrete content direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily kitchen or garden work | Warm, specific, lightly humorous | Teasing over technique, pride in a specialty, imperfect but kind cooperation |
| New arrival | Attentive, cautious or welcoming according to scenario | Needs, names, practical help and negotiated belonging before exposition dumps |
| Festival | Communal, lively, varied | Shared preparation, performers, food, games and music; no required humiliation or forced eating |
| Expedition | Curious, physically grounded | Weather, route decisions, provisions, small acts of help and personal voice |
| Danger | Serious and readable | Short urgent information, visible stakes, clear decisions; avoid undercutting grief with immediate slapstick |
| Loss | Restrained, particular, continuing | Named absence, changed work or space, memorial practices and relationships that persist |
| Rare wonder | Meaningful, interpretively open in this game | Limited encounters with consequences or emotional weight; source certainty may be deliberately adapted |

Sound can express productive space: tools, water, footfalls, hearths, food preparation, birds and distant communal signals. Music can distinguish a march, work song, feast, tale or memorial. These are authored sound-direction categories; no new audio channel count, mix level or looping schedule is set by the books.

## 6. Conflict without flattening the world

The sources contain compassionate acts by unexpected people and severe prejudice, cruelty and collective punishment. Both belong in the analysis. The accepted game policy should assess behavior and scenario history rather than assign immutable morality from species. A faction can be dangerous because of leadership, coercive institutions, material demands or individual acts; its members need not all share one motive.

Serious consequences should remain visible through wounded survivors, changed duties, missing people, memorial objects, migration and damaged homes. That does not authorize source torture or cruelty toward children as playable content. The existing content-boundary decision still governs, including unresolved offscreen treatment. Under the existing bible §12.5 interim guard, exclude the named subjects from new game depictions, backstory, implied threats and offscreen events until clarified. This broader guard is the existing agent interpretation, not an additional user answer.

Source conquest outcomes also differ. Some households return home; allies retain separate leadership; enslaved people are freed; some antagonists flee or change occupation. Use those possibilities to avoid assuming that successful play must annex every neighbor or erase every defeated group.

## 7. EARS direction checks

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| FEEL-001 | When a scenario introduces danger, its opening shall establish specific residents, routines or places whose disruption matters. |
| FEEL-002 | When a new asset family is planned, the brief shall include its ordinary-life use, material reference and relevant source record. |
| FEEL-003 | When a character loses an ability or role, the scenario shall identify continuing agency, assistance or a meaningful alternate role where the accepted systems support it. |
| FEEL-004 | When a feast is selected, its plan shall identify occasion, participating households, preparation and social purpose rather than use a generic celebration label alone. |
| FEEL-005 | When dialogue adopts a source voice, the writer shall use light readable dialect and distinct personal knowledge rather than equate accent with intelligence. |
| FEEL-006 | When source humor depends on coercion or cruelty that conflicts with the user's boundaries, the game adaptation shall change the event and record that change. |
| FEEL-007 | When a rare-wonder event is authored, its brief shall distinguish the source evidence from the game's selected uncertainty. |
| FEEL-008 | When a scenario ends, its authored aftermath shall account for named losses, changed homes, alliances and continuing community life. |

These checks guide future specifications. They do not invent unresolved family simulation, diplomacy, morale, quest or sound-system algorithms.
