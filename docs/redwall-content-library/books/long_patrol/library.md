## Direction that should survive implementation

Everyday care gives defense its meaning: cooking, nursing, shared work, household space and retirement remain substantial roles. The book explicitly questions reckless commanders and premature enlistment. Its ending retains Tammo’s limp, Cregga’s blindness, changed careers and grief across seasons. Treat these as long-term character states with continuing agency.

The strongest building references are Camp Tussock’s stockade and communal cellar, Waterhog raft households, adaptive owl housing, and the buried Kotir/Redwall overlap. All movement remains spatially connected. Source improvised shoring, chain swings, flame effects and healing are not validated engineering or medical instructions.
