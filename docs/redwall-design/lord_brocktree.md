# Lord Brocktree — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-LB-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

The framing household makes a martial institution domestic: Russano carries research in a borrowed pail, Rosalaun teases him, children interrupt and a public reading begins over bread and cheese. His account credits gardens, schools, laws and comfortable rooms alongside defense. A mountain stronghold's identity therefore needs places for learning, meals and family life as well as a forge. [LB-D01–02]

Dotti's theatrical confidence and Brocktree's immense presence coexist with hospitality from people of very different sizes. The mole cavern is a real home in her perception, complete with colored lanterns, woven mats, root cupboards and household beetles. These details are exceptionally useful for art direction: the setting's warmth is an arrangement of usable things, soft light and social attention. [LB-D04–05]

Ruff's fishing lesson explicitly values restraint in taking life for food. Later coalition meals name each group's contribution and relieve sentries so everyone can attend. Scarcity, provisioning and celebration belong in the same design language. Neither apparently abundant food nor a narrative feast supplies an infinite-resource rule. [LB-D03, LB-D08]

## Location and traversal direction

The mole cavern is warm and established; the sea-otter cave is a shelter behind a movable bush by a storm-beaten cliff; the mountain contains both comfortable public life and difficult forgotten approaches. Use different light, materials and sound to communicate these purposes without assigning all caves one mood. [LB-D04, LB-D06]

The confined tunnel forces single-file travel and body-specific posture; Brocktree crawls where others bend. A later hole is compared against his width. This supports the adopted requirement for body-plus-gear clearance and an explicit traversal profile. The story supplies no metric dimensions or movement speeds. Its tide-dependent hazards do not authorize unspecifed flooding or drowning. [LB-D09]

Konul's underwater work around boats demonstrates precise interaction with objects while submerged, separate from moving through water. The ordinary movement foundation must distinguish arrival at an underwater work location from permission to perform a particular task there. Tactical disruption of vessels remains future work. [LB-D07]

## Wonder and further direction

The ancestral chamber combines carved history, absence, scent and visions. Record this as meaningful narrative experience without selecting a pharmacological or supernatural truth for the game. Source methods are not instructions for ritual mechanics. Russano's closing exchange presents history as continuing responsibility, a useful direction for scenario epilogues that return to everyday life rather than end only on a victory screen. [LB-D10–11]
