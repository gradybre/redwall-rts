# Mossflower — a living woodland assembled through cooperation

`DESIGN-READ-MF-001` · Separate design-reading pass · Targeted primary-text evidence. Focus: community identity, material culture, space and sensory direction. Source locators refer to the supplied extraction; conversion errors remain possible.

## Themes, feeling and narrative style

The woodland is a **network of people who bring different capacities and provisions**. Bella supplies an established home; others bring food, scouting, swimming, excavation, lifting and knowledge. That is a strong design foundation for a colony where a completed place records several people's work. Avoid making a specialist a passive buff attached to a building: show what they actually contribute and who takes over when they rest. [MF-D03–05, MF-D10]

Gonff's songs are situated social acts: improvised self-celebration, affectionate teasing and distraction during anxious work. His claim that a newly composed chant is ancient turns tradition into something people actively make. The game's original community customs can likewise develop from repeated use. A song should have an occasion, singer and listeners; background music need not imitate a literal singing resident. [MF-D01–02, MF-D06, MF-D08]

Brockhall gives underground life comfort and social differentiation. Clay color, carved roots, chairs, nursery, stores and a secondary exit make it a home with history, not a generic cave. It is especially valuable for the adopted movement direction: the same underground place hosts private rest, communal eating, care, planning and circulation. [MF-D04]

The text changes scale from humming bees and food smells to a monumental forge and dangerous river passages. Preserve that contrast through framing, sound and usable space. A rare impressive room feels larger when most tasks occur at domestic scale. This is a composition principle, not a new species-height multiplier. [MF-D04, MF-D09–10]

## Objects, craft and sensory cues

The green jerkin, broad belt, flask, dagger and flute make Gonff recognizable through a small practical kit. Bella's unsorted scrolls mix recipes, records and woodland knowledge; a secret drawer and ancient script show that records need finding and interpretation. Quill-and-chart work makes geographical knowledge into an activity. [MF-D01, MF-D07–08]

The most direct additional movement evidence is cooperative engineering: otters dig from underwater toward floodgates, moles handle earth, and squirrels are asked to rig tree hoists for rocks. This validates the **direction** of useful everyday skills crossing movement domains. It supplies no automatic species multiplier, underwater duration or hoist capacity. [MF-D10]

For atmosphere, use bees, wind/leaf motion, muffled underground rooms, food smells conveyed through action/prose, flute practice and the forge's hiss. A public work area can be lively without every resident emitting sound every frame. Keep sound events and material changes tied to actual visible work.
