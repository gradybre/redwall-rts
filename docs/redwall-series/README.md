## 1. What this package establishes

Brendan supplied readable files for eleven novels. Each receives an individual study below. The seven titles outside the originally named six-book focus are meaningful additional primary sources; they do not displace that focus without a new decision. The expanded evidence allows comparison across all six focus titles only at mixed levels: complete supplied-text reading for *Lord Brocktree*, targeted passage readings for four others and the previously inspected official excerpts for *Eulalia!*. Its complete text has not been supplied in the verified files.

The main finding is that Redwall's distinctive community life is made through relationships expressed in practical acts. Food is prepared, given, remembered and sometimes withheld. Buildings receive strangers, preserve mistakes and change uses. Leaders need help, make contested decisions, teach successors and remain members of households. Grief changes daily life without preventing every later joke or celebration. These patterns provide a stronger common foundation for plans and implementation than a catalog of species, weapons and picturesque rooms. They remain bounded literary interpretations, supported by the individual studies.

The books also disagree with one another and with the project's adopted choices. This package preserves those differences. A source can provide a powerful image while its social assumptions, violence or implied physics remain unsuitable for direct implementation.

## 3. Comparative findings

### SERIES-F01 — Home is a practice before it is a building

*Mossflower* gives displaced people shelter within Brockhall and shows collective planning before the Abbey exists. *Salamandastron* makes care depend on laundry, water, rest and retained knowledge. *Triss* depicts a new household learning from the kindness its members encountered elsewhere. *Lord Brocktree* similarly places domestic provision beside military founding. A community's visual style alone cannot carry these relationships. [MF-P04–06; SA-P03–05; TR-P07; earlier LB study]

For Rowan's Refuge, the current experienced-mouse-keeper choice fits this emphasis, but it does not answer who provided the furnished hall, where the initial stores came from or how the displaced residents know one another. These remain authored-history decisions. Do not silently choose a novel's tragic opening as the answer. A credible beginning needs a short chain of prior acts that explains the exact existing initialization, not a new population or resource bundle.

### SERIES-F02 — Feasts have different social jobs

The studies distinguish welcome, relief, memorial, succession, harvest, reunion, liberation and ordinary companionship. Tansy delays celebration until harvest is secured. Cregga's feast is a chosen farewell. Riftgard's banquet changes who can eat food their labor produced. The final *Redwall* feast accommodates different memories of the same event. [PL-P08; TG-P05–06; TR-P07; RW-P09]

This supports the user's decision to include several feast meanings. It does not support a single always-positive effect. A feast's authoring brief should state its occasion, participants, work, absences and consequences. Numerical effects, ingredient costs and scheduling require the owning economy specification. Ordinary community life also needs modest meals and unexceptional routines; otherwise every meal becomes a climax and rare wonder loses contrast.

### SERIES-F03 — Leadership is plural and morally uneven

Observed routes to authority include Mortimer's appointments, CORIM's deliberation, Cregga's long preparation of a successor, Dippler's revenge-linked custom, badger lineage, prophetic recognition, Humble's negotiated acceptance and refusal of royal crowns. These cannot honestly be collapsed into one canonical government system. [RW-P08; MF-P03; TG-P05; MX-P07–08; SA-P08; RT-P01; RT-P07]

Some leaders also withhold information, prejudge outsiders, improvise reassurance or use humiliation. Their kindness elsewhere does not turn those practices into project requirements. The appropriate game question is what responsibilities and limits each scenario gives the player's role. The already chosen scenario-specific role and locally authored Hearth Charter remain distinct from Abbey appointment or badger sovereignty.

### SERIES-F04 — Courage is not one occupation

Brome's bravery survives his rejection of killing. Russa tells Tammo that fighting ability does not compel a military life. Humble remains attached to cellar work while leading. Rimrose takes up recording later in life. Sunflash becomes a farmer and poet while retaining defensive readiness. [MW-P03–04; LP-P04; RT-P01; MX-P09; OC-P08]

These examples support varied lives and changing responsibilities. They do not authorize a new personality simulator or an unrestricted profession system. Existing job definitions and entity schemas still decide what the runtime can represent. For present scene writing, avoid equating visual species, age, gender or current role with a person's entire competence and ambition.

### SERIES-F05 — Mercy and source morality require separate ledgers

Romsca's care relationship, peaceful water rats, Julian Gingivere and Tagg's refusals provide specific counterexamples to simplistic moral categories. *Outcast* also includes Veil's wrongdoing, prior suspicion and a contested final act. The narrators and sympathetic characters often use species-coded language or approve severe treatment of enemies. [PL-P04; MX-P09; RW-P06; TG-P02–03; OC-P01–06]

Consequently, DEC-005 is an intentional game choice supported by selected possibilities in the series, not a claim that the source is consistently species-neutral. No newcomer should carry an automatically correct moral verdict in their species, appearance, name or undisclosed ancestry. Conversely, individual accountability should not disappear merely because the game rejects inherited guilt. Exact crime, exile or prisoner mechanics remain unapproved and are not implied by these observations.

### SERIES-F06 — Grief survives success and does not have one outward form

Martin's bodily recovery precedes any clear social recovery. Tammo weeps after victory and retains an injury. Grath's arrow becomes a keepsake when she starts a life elsewhere. Nimbalo asks for privacy before returning to public humor. Kroova mourns a planned shared meal that will never happen. Sunflash continues to remember Skarlath in a cultivated place. [MW-P07; LP-P07–08; PL-P06; TG-P04; TR-P04; OC-P08]

This supports serious consequences and community memory while resisting a single numerical grief curve as an explanation of every person. The project still needs exact rules if grief will affect authoritative state. Until then, use this evidence to write consistent original scenes and prop histories, not to invent state transitions, recovery timers, combat modifiers or diagnostic labels.

### SERIES-F07 — Objects gain meaning through custody and use

The sword can carry inheritance and labor; a bell can move from public timekeeping to violence and remembrance; a tapestry can preserve collective history and warrant recovery. Pearls can be relinquished, crowns refused, an axe repurposed, a ship renamed and a living cutting tended. [MF-P07; RW-P05, RW-P07–09; MX-P04–05; PL-P07; RT-P07; TR-P04; MW-P09]

No universal “precious equals corrupt” rule follows. Ask what the object does in relationships: whose work made it, who claims it, whom it remembers and what keeping or discarding it changes. That is the information an asset agent needs before adding a conspicuous emblem. It also distinguishes a useful visual variant from a new item system.

### SERIES-F08 — Wonder has several evidential forms

Boar's carvings and the books' dream-guided appointments receive real narrative weight. Mokkan's concealment has a material association with clothing and cover. The Walking Stone is identified as a tortoise. *Lord Brocktree* includes explained spectacles alongside consequential visions. None of these findings establishes a complete universal metaphysics. [MF-P07; MX-P05, MX-P08; PL-P07; RT-P03–04; earlier LB study]

The user's rare, meaningful and uncertain wonder is therefore a deliberate design selection. Preserve the emotional and social stakes of a dream without requiring a hidden truth flag. A particular puzzle may receive an ordinary explanation while another experience remains unresolved. Do not turn “uncertain” into repetitive fake-outs, or make every local custom a reliable resource exploit.

### SERIES-F09 — The world is connected without requiring assimilation

Gingivere leaves after being welcomed. Grath settles at Ruddaring. Freed people in *Marlfox* choose different destinations. Sleeve and Kroova create a home at Riftgard. Tergen stays at Salamandastron. These are successful outcomes even when the Abbey does not retain every friend. [MF-P06; PL-P06; MX-P09; TR-P07; RT-P07]

The Refuge should have a relationship to a wider world and to residents' prior homes. However, departure mechanics, campaign travel and immigration policy are separate implementation choices. For now, record intended relationships in scenario context and preserve the approved admission process rather than inventing autonomous population flows.

### SERIES-F10 — History is made by narrators with positions

The recorders, travelers, family storytellers and letters are part of the series' world. Ellayo distinguishes hearsay from experience; Martin chooses what to tell; Rosabel collects accounts; Sleeve thanks people she has never met because their actions reached her. These are different knowledge positions. [MX-P03; MW-P08–09; TG-P01; TR-P06–07]

Claude should never treat a character's confidence as automatically equivalent to canonical fact or current game state. A scene may preserve a mistaken belief, but the player's operational UI must still truthfully report the state required by its owning specification. Lore uncertainty cannot justify misleading stock counts, deaths, selection state or warnings.

## 5. Art, sound and settlement-space guidance

Status: `SOURCE_SUPPORTED` for cited observations; `PROPOSED` for the original production method below. No asset budget, rig, shader, building footprint or audio implementation is changed.

### 5.1 Space must explain a routine

Use the following **functional graph**, not a measured canonical floor plan:

```text
                 arrival / return / local testimony
                               |
                               v
                       welcome and orientation
                          /             \
                         v               v
                rest / private care    shared company
                         ^               ^      |
                         |               |      v
                  clean supplies     prepare -> serve -> clear
                         ^               ^                 |
                         |               |                 v
                  water / laundry     stores <------- clean / return
                                         |
                                         v
                                  next ordinary day
```

The return loop concerns reusable serving items. The graph expresses dependencies and movement motives. The authorized building layout and pathfinding contracts determine actual routes. Do not invent walls or stairs from it.

| Space family | Literary emphasis | Original brief must settle |
|---|---|---|
| Abbey | Shared halls, archives, care, stores, bells, gardens and changing room use | Who uses the room, at what time, with which existing objects and routes |
| Developed burrow/root hall | Shared refuge, protected stores, social gathering and established hospitality | Approved construction method, access, material supports and room connections |
| Temporary bank shelter | Scarcity, weather protection, improvised food and fire | Reason for occupation and visible difference from a permanent home |
| Holt/river settlement | Water access, travel, food and family memory | Approved species/anatomy and navigation interfaces; no invented hydrology |
| Mountain fortress | Training, forging, hospitality, lineage and succession | Scenario era, residents, domestic rooms and relationship to duty |
| Reclaimed settlement | Changed ownership, access to stores, new names and care after arrival | Allowed history, current authority and practical continuity after the founding moment |

### 5.2 Props, bodies and voices

Give a focal prop one grounded reason to exist before adding lore. A scarred ladle, reworked bench or repaired woven panel can suggest use when its scene explains that use. Do not cover every surface with memorials or heroic symbols. An exceptional item needs ordinary surroundings for contrast. [RW-P01, RW-P05; SA-P07; MW-P09; TG-P09]

Body variety must remain distinct from moral judgment. Several inspected passages mock weight, speech or appearance; this is source framing, not an art mandate. Light dialect should distinguish social voices without making instructions difficult to read. Warm humor can arise from familiarity, interrupted dignity, disputed recipe names and affectionate exaggeration. Avoid lifting comic threats against children or captive humiliation into the game under the same label. [RT-P06–07; TG-P07; MX-P08]

Songs can be intimate, communal, commemorative, boastful or political. Their function matters more than constant playback. Use original writing and composition. The user's blended acoustic, environmental and orchestral direction remains the music policy; no recorded performance, licensed soundtrack or full voice cast is approved by this research.

### 5.3 Blender/MCP handoff boundaries

This package does not create meshes. Every later asset brief must reference the existing crowd-rendering asset conventions for scale, axes, export and budgets. Use the 1.0 m mouse anchor; do not scale from a horse, a PDF illustration or a narrator's comparison. Determine a burrow's construction type before modeling the entrance. A cutaway or visible stair implies geometry and navigation that must already have an owning specification. A ceremonial object must name its scenario and provenance; do not add a canonical insignia to an original Refuge asset by habit.

## 6. Static authoring schema and validation rules

This is a **documentation schema**, newly authored here. It is not an ECS component, save format or approved runtime subsystem. All records remain static Markdown/YAML/JSON until an owning feature spec explicitly defines another representation.

| Field | Type / allowed values | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| `record_id` | String matching `SERIES-[A-Z]+[0-9]{2}` | Unique within this package |
| `record_kind` | `OBSERVATION`, `INTERPRETATION`, `ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL` | Exactly one value |
| `evidence_refs` | Nonempty list of registered passage IDs | Every ID resolves to this audit or the earlier LB audit |
| `claim` | Nonempty prose string | One assessable claim; distinguish speech from narrated action |
| `evidence_limit` | Nonempty prose string | Must name any incomplete sequence or uncertain inference |
| `game_policy_refs` | List of existing `DEC-nnn` IDs | Every referenced decision exists; empty allowed only for pure observations |
| `implementation_status` | `REFERENCE_ONLY`, `DRAFT_REQUIRES_SPEC`, `ALREADY_OWNED_BY_SPEC` | Research alone cannot select the third value |
| `mechanical_owner` | Repository-relative document path or `null` | `null` required where no runtime feature exists |
| `departures` | List of strings | Explicitly record any game/source difference; empty means none asserted |

Completed example:

```yaml
record_id: SERIES-EX01
record_kind: ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL
evidence_refs: [SA-P07, MW-P09, TR-P04]
claim: An original community memorial may be a useful or tended object with a known recipient and history.
evidence_limit: The sources offer different practices; no single Refuge memorial custom has been selected.
game_policy_refs: [DEC-011, DEC-014, DEC-022]
implementation_status: DRAFT_REQUIRES_SPEC
mechanical_owner: null
departures:
  - The object and its history must be original to the selected scenario rather than assigned a novel character's loss.
  - No mood bonus, inventory recipe or new memory component is implied.
```

## 9. Provenance and verification

Inherited: the six-book focus, expanded user-provided corpus, source order, current gameplay constants, ECS direction, content limits and confirmed DEC choices. Newly authored: all critical interpretations, finding/contradiction IDs, editorial schema, EARS handoff rules, scene-production recommendations and interview candidates. Measured: file sizes, SHA-256 digests, extracted counts, inspected-range counts and coverage ratios. Source-only numbers include novel dates, seasons, named groups and literary quantities; none becomes a gameplay constant here.

Coverage formula, using whitespace-delimited tokens after the documented extraction normalization:

| Quantity | Definition |
|---|---|
| `W_total` | Sum of token counts over every extracted nonempty block |
| `R` | Union of all inspected inclusive block intervals for that file |
| `W_inspected` | Sum of token counts for blocks whose IDs belong to `R` |
| `coverage_percent` | `100 * W_inspected / W_total`, displayed to two decimal places; null only if `W_total = 0` |

The source audit contains actual file basenames and archive/PDF locators but no full narrative text. EPUBs were inspected in spine order for extraction; PDF locations use one-based physical pages; the unencrypted MOBI was decoded before passage inspection. PDF text was spot-checked visually at opening and later pages. Extraction artifacts remain possible, so quotations, disputed spelling and edition-specific claims need a verified edition. The package intentionally paraphrases rather than reproducing chapters, poems or dialogue collections.
