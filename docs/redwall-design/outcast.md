# Outcast of Redwall — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-OC-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

This pass adds a domestic counterweight to the earlier study's difficult questions about Veil. Sunflash and Skarlath participate in a household's comfort, seasonal craft and friendship. Skarlath helps Lully make cheese and takes pride in the result. Their companionship is expressed through shared expertise, not merely battle assistance. A bird needs opportunities to belong through work, tastes and relationships as well as reconnaissance. [OC-D02–03]

The source remains morally uncomfortable. Bryony's loyalty, Veil's actual wrongdoing and the community's prejudgment must stay distinct; see earlier OC-P01–06. This pass does not turn the novel into an endorsement of the game's adopted species-neutral morality. It offers useful content while preserving that disagreement.

Two linked images are especially strong: a weathered mountain containing domestic life, and remembered founders inhabiting present-day rituals. Meriam's tribute names people who built the community; it makes its architecture a result of humanlike effort and memory. Original game customs can honor named contributors without reproducing the novel's grace or claiming a universal religious rule. [OC-D01, OC-D06–07]

## Areas and movement

The mountain opening names halls, caverns, corridors, chambers and tunnels, with a ledge picnic beside an entrance. Underground does not mean socially isolated. The later watery cavern gives a very different experience: echoing falls, a broad internal river, stepping-stone rocks and reflected moving light. Keep underground and submerged space distinct. Standing on a dry rock inside a cave is not diving. [OC-D01, OC-D09]

Firjak's remembered injury limits climbing, after which he learns swimming. This is unusually useful evidence against fixed species ability locks: an individual changes practical capacities over a life. It does not define rehabilitation duration or an injury progression rule. Existing accepted movement should consult a versioned individual body/training/gear profile, and future profiles must be authored explicitly. [OC-D04]

## Additional implementation direction

Give a keepsake both a material description and an associated relationship. Distinguish a gifted emblem's recognized meaning from an actual control permission. Record the cheese preparation as a content proposal compatible with the dietary direction but absent from current recipes. Do not label every vaguely named cream or cheese in all books plant-derived on the strength of this one explicit example.
