The connector's final funding guard follows the last Recipe or endpoint
observer and precedes the actual Inventory commit. It must prove the exact

with no new bill observer after the refund has committed.

Contacts are a typed fail-closed dependency. Admission feasibility, current

permission. A complete runtime owner must supply concrete source-bound contact
