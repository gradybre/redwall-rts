# Triss — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-TR-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

Freedom is given a material future: food shared, homes chosen, work resumed and institutions helped from outside their own walls. The earlier study records Sleeve's perspective on rebuilding after liberation. This pass adds the cultivated island, domestic travel and the continuity of community roles. The player should encounter people with futures and preferences, not simply rescued units entering storage. [TR-D06, TR-D08; earlier TR-P07]

Skipper's dawn routine is a precise model for everyday community presence: a pond swim, walltop exercise, practice, then a quietly prepared breakfast tray. The cooks know he does not want conversation first thing. He chooses a sunlit spot to eat and reflect. This is character expressed through a routine and others' accommodation. Use this approach for Rowan and named residents without copying the exact sequence to everyone. [TR-D04]

Scarum's extravagance, Raura's pointed songs and the elders' affectionate teasing provide varied humor. They also show where humor can become coercive or repetitive. Preserve warm familiarity while avoiding constant mockery, permanent gluttony or intrusive commentary during grief. Different registers should be audible without using accent as a moral marker.

## Areas, map and movement

Redwall's everyday Cavern Hole differs from the more ceremonial Great Hall. In warm weather residents anticipate moving meals into the orchard. Give rooms more than one temporally appropriate use, with real access to serving areas. The main building's gables, stained glass and worn floors read differently in dawn light than under feast lanterns. [TR-D01–02, TR-D04]

Peace Island is characterized by productive slopes, a gathering plateau and horticultural competitions. Distinguish it visually through cultivation patterns and communal judging displays. Raura's rocky water approach and the small craft's passage to open sea illustrate transport clearance and tides, not swimming navmesh. Migooch tents offer another portable domestic kit. [TR-D05–07]

## Additional implementation direction

A hero's departure needs a record of who covers existing responsibilities. A gifted/renamed ship should retain its persistent identity while its display name changes. A feast can have absentees and remembered friends without halting every work system. Preserve source context in content tables and reuse the existing event/job/presentation pipeline; do not create bespoke runtime classes for each named character.
