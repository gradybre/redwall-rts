# Pearls of Lutra — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-PL-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

The material world offers several kinds of value: an ordinary spoon opens a hidden compartment, a handmade arrow carries memory, and spectacular pearls can be worth less than a friend's safety. Craft, use and ownership make objects meaningful. Treat a treasure as an object with provenance and claims rather than only a rarity color and sale price. [PL-D02, PL-D05–06; earlier PL-P07]

This is also a strong reference for knowledgeable domestic life. Tansy gathers for a healer, bakes, observes rooms and reasons with Rollo and Martin. A tasting discussion becomes a written record. Intelligence is distributed across observation, practical handling and social exchange; solving a problem does not require a single omniscient scholar. [PL-D01, PL-D04–05, PL-D09]

Ruddaring presents welcome through gestures and courtesy: a visitor lays down a sword, an elder asks about origin, companions help carry belongings and conversation moves toward a meal. This is a useful rhythm for original first-contact scenes. It does not replace the project's adopted admission policy or automatically authorize disarming visitors. [PL-D07]

## Locations, maps and movement

Abbey circulation passes from public halls to dormitories and then neglected attic stairs. Dust, silence, stored furniture and a small high light source make familiar architecture newly mysterious. Use a change in occupation and maintenance, not arbitrary purple magical lighting, to distinguish an unused room. The source only provides qualitative geometry. [PL-D03]

Ruddaring lies within a mountainous crater setting with grass, cave dwelling, stream and limited sky. Holt Lutra's doorway becomes a tomb after Grath's work. These locations should carry different social meanings even if both use rock and water assets. Reusing a cave kit must not erase the difference between refuge, working home and memorial. [PL-D02, PL-D07–08]

Sampetra's timber control, established in the earlier study, is useful campaign-economy context: a repair dependency creates political leverage. It supplies no prices, growth rate or production budget. Do not transplant its treeless island condition into every coast.

## Further direction

Delay a harvest celebration until storage is secure when the scenario calls for it; the source explicitly does so. Record who made a dish and how a recipe traveled. Preserve debate and sensory judgment in original flavor text, while all actual inventory, consumption and recipe math stays in the balance catalog. Dreams receive attributed narrators; uncertain wonder never justifies misleading operational UI.
