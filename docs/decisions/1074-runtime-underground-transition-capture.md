## Decision

Extend the reviewed offline capture through a separate driver. Reuse the
existing imported-skin evaluator and native palette comparison without changing
it. Drive actual DemoActor fades, ResidentBrain turns, tunnel reversal and exit
methods, and DemoClock substeps. Record each observed state, root transform,
clip, clip time/rate, visible attachment and transition event. Synthetic route
fixtures are explicitly identified; their geometry does not become a connector
catalog, paid construction or production access permission.

Use real Residents, Jobs, ItemDefinitions, Inventory, Reservations, Gear and
HaulCarry in isolated identity fixtures. Readers require full live references,
actual owner/equipment agreement, real satchel ownership, carried lot and exact
quantity. They never derive a body, cargo shape or grip permission from a demo
index, generic item name or species. The logical adult rig key is reported
separately from the imported demo asset; missing physical variant bindings stay
explicit. Fixture creation is not a production work or material transaction.

Actual demo attachment visibility is measured as implemented, including hidden
frames during a carry fade. The driver does not force items visible or overwrite
the brain state in order to obtain an apparently complete envelope. Separate
fixtures measure one held prop at a time. Existing source speeds, blend times,
dig clocks and fit logic are reused; no new clearance, grip, recipe, labor or
movement constant is introduced.

Every source, inherited helper, catalog, imported scene and loaded clip is
hashed before and after a batch. Input/output bundles are create-only. Bounded
test schedules and reduced fixture arena sizes are engineering instrumentation,
not gameplay policy. Missing events, temporal observations, attachment states,
identity proofs or native palette samples refuse a claimed successful capture.

## Production closure sequence

The 1024u/m convention, actual imported scales, authored clip rates/fades, item
masses and immutable excavation datum already have source owners. Connector
silhouettes, integer tread/rail geometry and material mapping can be authored as
engineering content once the required body/state set has a qualified envelope.
Fixed variants should contain that envelope plus its explicit contact/recovery
volume; outward integer conversion cannot be replaced by a guessed margin.

Tighter continuous source bounds can subdivide actual joint interpolation and
blend intervals, then compose bounded spring/stoop and attachment transforms.
Native samples are adversarial observations against that calculation, never a
substitute for its residual proof. A useful bound must cover the actual runtime
modifier and fitting semantics; the earlier whole-skeleton sphere is too loose
to select production stair dimensions.

Required load/gear permissions, unsupported grip/slope capabilities, missing
recovery states, standard level spacing and allowed local height offsets need concrete authored
contracts. The approved planning slice assigns spacing, profiles and offsets
to engineering; these do not require a fresh policy decision merely because
they were previously unauthored. New grip/load permissions and balance recipes
remain separate from geometry, UV and measurement engineering. This increment leaves those gaps visible and
keeps all output marked sampled and unqualified.
