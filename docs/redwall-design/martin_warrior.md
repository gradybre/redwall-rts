# Martin the Warrior — design, culture and material world

`DESIGN-READ-MW-001` · Revision 1.0 · 2026-09-06 · Separate targeted design reading. This is source evidence and interpretation, not a complete new sequential reading or an approved content expansion. See [package guide](README.md) for authority, measured coverage and catalog handling.

## Theme, style and feeling

Noonvale is first made desirable through someone's remembered experience: filtered dawn light, cookhouse smoke, dew, flowers and the food a parent prepares. This gives an absent home sensory specificity before a map displays it. For displaced residents, record what they miss in concrete terms without forcing them all to share the same lost-home story. [MW-D02]

Hospitality is also an expression of recovered agency. When freed people help prepare a meal, cooking is an ability they can exercise again. Preserve the names and skills of helpers; a liberation story is incomplete if beneficiaries become anonymous mouths to feed. The earlier MW-P03–09 discusses the limits of vengeance and the privacy of grief. This pass adds the everyday future that makes that loss matter. [MW-D05]

Rose's singing is socially and practically active. The bee episode is an unusual narrative event in which she proposes an explanation and acts on it. It supports a character whose voice matters, not an automatic music-based insect-control ability. The reader need not settle universal metaphysics for the game to value her resourcefulness. [MW-D04]

## Spatial reference and movement

Polleekin's dead oak leans against rock, making its trunk an accessible incline. A room sits between three boughs; driftwood and cordage form floor/roof; moss, earth and leaves seal gaps; woven foliage makes walls; a broad branch serves as seating. This is a direct model reference for constructed arboreal housing. It does not prove ordinary moles can perform a squirrel's unsupported climb. Route geometry, grip and body clearance decide access. [MW-D03]

Noonvale's orchard includes mixed fruit trees and berry rows separating it from vegetables. A dead sycamore becomes a communal work project and future stump seating beside the waterfall. This ties land clearance to named useful material and an intended amenity. Roots, leverage and team labor are source observations; the game still needs exact work/cost rules. [MW-D08–09]

## Further opportunities

Use season, occasion and individual taste to vary meals. A feast can welcome visitors, mark liberation or follow shared work. Home audio should include voices, water and utensils without filling every quiet scene with music. An inhabited tree, cave and hall can express the same value of welcome through different construction methods.
