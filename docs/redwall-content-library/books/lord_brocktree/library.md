All 3,560 normalized narrative blocks / 105,555 words inspected in eighteen sequential chunks. The collection-labeled source contains this novel only.

## Theme, style and game direction

The campaign is built from households that can feed, shelter, teach and mourn as well as fight. Frutch loses his home; rescue first removes elders and injured allies; a supply cave becomes a shared refuge with enlarged space, ovens, beds and a guarded entrance. Moles, hedgehogs, shrews, squirrels, otters and hares contribute different skills without becoming biological job locks. Rulango is a nonverbal thinking collaborator, not a transport pet.

Dotti earns formal authority through initiative and care as well as combat. Bucko returns north while retaining alliance, allowing distinct political households to cooperate without universal annexation. The series voice moves between precise food texture, practical route problems, boasting, comic songs, generosity and consequential grief. Preserve warm humor through willing participation; the source eating contest also contains coercion that conflicts with the accepted game boundary.

War leaves named deaths, wounds, broken equipment, memorial gifts, leadership succession and changed homes. Jukka and Fleetscut are memorialized rather than reset. Mercy and release coexist in the text with revenge, prisoner abuse and no-quarter orders: document those conflicts and implement the accepted project values. Trunn is explicitly alive after the duel; his later implied drowning is a separate event.

## Spatial and material direction

Use the source cave sequence as a connected route: hidden fissure → pool ledge → enlarged descent opening → low passage → tidal sea cave → shore. Tide, body clearance, injury, load and available assistance alter traversal. The bird-assisted rescue uses carried line ends, fixed attachment points, hauling groups and rest stops; no flyer transports every person directly. A major badger must crawl or enlarge an opening rather than ignore its geometry.

The strongest asset references are the shuttered mountain fortress; tide-dark cave and pool reflections; root-supported mole and hedgehog households; willow/hornbeam/rock landmarks; rough sea-buckthorn refuge; sailcloth and moss beds; griddle stones, shell bowls, flagons, ladles and battered military heirlooms. Preserve recognizable animal anatomy and individual wear without making species a moral alignment.

## Food assembly and provenance

Ruff’s dace preparation at block 337 is unusually specific about coating, cooking water and serving vegetables. Frutch’s nut slice at 2317–2318 specifies a shortbread base, plum preserve, beech nuts and hazelnuts. Doomeye’s shield stew at 3253 specifies its contents and explicitly lacks pepperwort. The recipe file separates these facts from all authored completions.

Cheese and cream mentions here do not establish their biological origin. Their proposed oat counterparts are adaptations; Outcast’s separately attested greensap cheese must not be treated as proof for every book. Songs, remembered dishes and deliberately pretended shrimp dishes are useful authoring references, but not evidence that those meals were served.
